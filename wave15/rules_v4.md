# Wave 15 qualification rules (v4, agreed 13 Oct 2026)

Discovery net: Clay revenue USD 1B+; Clay revenue USD 150M-1B; Apollo total funding USD 100M+.

Filter 1 (scale) passes on ANY of:
- Valuation / market cap USD 1B+ -> can be Yes
- USD 100M+ cumulative equity with a priced round in the last 36 months -> can be Yes
- Annual revenue USD 500M+ -> can be Yes
- Annual revenue USD 150M-500M -> capped at Stretch
Otherwise (revenue < USD 150M, not USD 1B valued, not funded) -> NEAR-MISS tab.

Filters 2-5 unchanged from v3: 51+ staff; no distress; no F1 tie direct or via controlling parent (FAIL only when a tie IS found); not sanctioned (1260H / Entity List / UFLPA incl. subsidiaries).
Labels, fit score and priority formula unchanged from v3.
