# F1 Sponsorship Prospecting — Filter Spec

Screening criteria for generating F1 sponsorship prospect batches. This is the full, current version; it supersedes all previous versions.

Updated: 2026-09-18

## Hard filters (must pass all)

1. **Headcount:** 51 to 10,000+
2. **Revenue:** above USD 100M, or ARR above USD 50M growing more than 50% year on year. Valuation and funding do not count as revenue.
3. **Valuation:** market cap, post-money valuation or enterprise value above USD 1B. Family-owned companies with no published valuation pass on the revenue test alone; flag them.
4. **Capacity to spend:** public companies profitable or clearly improving and not in cost-cutting; venture-backed companies raised within 18 months for go-to-market or expansion, not capex or R&D; pre-revenue companies excluded.
5. **Audience:** sells to thousands of customers, not a handful. Exclude companies dependent on roughly 20 customers or fewer.
6. **No existing F1 ties:** not already sponsoring an F1 team, F1, a driver or a Grand Prix, directly or via parent.
7. **No duplicates:** not already in the master document, previous batches or the live pipeline.

## Soft filters (record and flag)

8. Flag anything valued above 50x revenue.
9. PE-owned four years or more: flag.
10. **Strategic fit** — score at least 2 of 4:
    - sells in three or more F1 markets;
    - buyers are affluent 25 to 54 or senior decision makers;
    - brand story maps to performance, technology, precision or global ambition;
    - category not already taken on the grid.

## Output per company

Name, HQ, ownership type, what it sells and to whom, headcount band, revenue with date and source, valuation with date, latest raise and use of funds, fit score, verdict (Yes / Stretch / No) with one-line reason, best angle. Mark estimates.

## Run

Batches of 50, one sector per batch. Attach the master document and exclude every name in it before generating.
