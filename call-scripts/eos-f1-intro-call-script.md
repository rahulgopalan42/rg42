## Boilerplate: what EOS does

**About EOS:** EOS is a family-owned German company, based in Krailling near Munich, that makes industrial 3D printers for metal and plastic parts, along with the powders, software and engineering support that go with them. It was founded in 1989 by Dr. Hans J. Langer and has been run since 2019 by his daughter, CEO Marie Niehaus-Langer; it says it has around 1,250 employees and installed its 5,000th industrial printer in January 2025. Its customers are manufacturers that need complex, high-value parts in aerospace and space, defense, energy, medical and automotive, along with the contract manufacturers who serve them, and its published case studies include MTU Aero Engines, ArianeGroup and Siemens Energy. The wider family-owned group includes AMCM, which builds its large-format machines, and the Austrian powder maker Metalpine, which EOS took full ownership of in April 2026. It doesn't publish revenue, and it says it's heading towards metal printing in full series production, with its new M4 ONYX machines, more materials such as titanium and aluminium, and a bigger manufacturing footprint in North America.

**In one line, if someone asks on the call:** "They make the industrial 3D printers companies use to print real metal and plastic parts, from aircraft engine parts to medical implants."
