## Boilerplate: what TII does

- **About TII:** The Technology Innovation Institute is the applied research arm of Abu Dhabi's Advanced Technology Research Council (ATRC), the government body that oversees advanced technology research in the emirate. Set up in 2020, it runs nine research centres covering AI and digital science, quantum, cryptography, secure systems, autonomous robotics, propulsion and space, advanced materials, renewable energy and directed energy. It's best known for Falcon, its family of open AI models that anyone can download, which TII says have been downloaded more than 55 million times; in 2026 it added a small Falcon reasoning model it says matches or beats much bigger ones, plus Falcon Perception and Falcon-Emirati, built for the Emirati dialect. Its work reaches into space too: in October 2026 it completed the landing probe for the UAE's mission to the asteroid belt, which TII says launches in 2028. TII is government-funded, led by CEO Dr Najwa Aaraj, and says its direction is "sovereign" AI and taking research out of the lab into products, partnerships and spin-out companies.
- **In one line, if someone asks on the call:** "They're Abu Dhabi's government-backed research institute. They make the Falcon AI models that anyone can download and use, and they work on things like quantum, robotics and space."
