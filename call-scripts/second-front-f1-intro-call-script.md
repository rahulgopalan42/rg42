## Boilerplate: what Second Front does

- **About Second Front:** Second Front Systems (2F) is a US software company and public benefit corporation that helps software makers get their products securely built, approved and running in government and other regulated environments. Its main product, 2F Game Warden, is a hosted platform that already holds top-tier US government cloud security approvals, so a company that deploys onto it inherits much of that work instead of starting from scratch; Second Front says this takes under 120 days on average, where the process can otherwise take up to two years and cost millions, as reported. Alongside it sit 2F Workshop, a secure development toolkit, and 2F Frontier, which delivers approved software to devices and remote sites. The company names software firms such as Cohere, Onebrief and Decision Lens among its customers, and AWS, Microsoft and Google Cloud as platform partners. It has raised about $150 million, most recently a $70 million Series C led by Salesforce Ventures in September 2024, and on 26 October 2026 it announced an authorization from the US Department of Veterans Affairs, with Nick Woodruff, its General Manager for North America, saying Game Warden "is built for any regulated environment, from defense to civilian missions."
- **In one line:** If someone asks on the call: "They help software companies build their products safely and get them approved to run for the government and other places with strict rules. Think of it as a secure production line for software, with the safety checks built in from the start, so it gets cleared in months, not years."
