## Boilerplate: what Aridge does

**About Aridge:** Aridge is the flying car company in the XPeng group, based in Guangzhou in southern China. It started in 2013 as Huitian under founder Zhao Deli, came under XPeng in 2020 and renamed itself from XPeng AeroHT to Aridge in October 2025, and according to CnEVPost it's majority-owned by XPeng and XPeng's chairman He Xiaopeng. Its first product, the Land Aircraft Carrier, is a six-wheeled road vehicle that carries, charges and launches a detachable two-seat electric aircraft, priced at no more than RMB 2 million (about $280,000) in China, and it says it has around 7,000 pre-orders, including 600 signed by Gulf dealer groups in Dubai in October 2025. It has raised about $1 billion in equity, most recently nearly $200 million in March 2026, and reports say it filed confidentially for a Hong Kong listing in January 2026. It's moving into series production at its own Guangzhou factory, targeting first deliveries in China by the end of 2026 (reports put full-scale deliveries in 2027) and Gulf sales from 2027, with a six-seat long-range model, the A868, in development.

**In one line, if someone asks on the call:** "They make a flying car that travels in the back of its own six-wheeled vehicle. You drive somewhere nice, the aircraft comes out, and you fly."
