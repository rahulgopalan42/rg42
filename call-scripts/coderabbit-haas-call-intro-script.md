# CodeRabbit x Haas x SPORTFIVE: Call Intro Script

**Call type:** Introduction call, intro only. The job of this opening: thank everyone, give Mark the context from your conversations with Pete, set a simple goal for the call, then run the introductions guests first (Pete, Mark), SPORTFIVE last, and hand the floor to Pete.

**How to use:** Say it like you talk; these lines are the shape, not a text to read. The whole opening should take about two minutes including everyone's hellos.

**Fill in before the call:**

- Confirm who's who: this script assumes Pete is from CodeRabbit, Mark is from Haas, and Bjorn and Knut are from SPORTFIVE
- Confirm Pete's preferred name (Pete or Peter) and Mark's role at Haas
- Have the CodeRabbit discovery script open for anything that comes up after the intro

---

## The intro

> "Hi everyone, thank you all for your time, really appreciate it. Glad we managed to get this one in the books.
>
> Mark, just to give you a bit of context: I've been speaking with Pete, and from our conversations it felt like there's something here really worth exploring with Haas.
>
> So the goal today is simple. Get to know each other a bit better, and see how Haas could support CodeRabbit.
>
> Before we dive in, let's do a quick round of introductions. Pete, would you mind kicking us off? Then Mark, and we'll round it off with the SPORTFIVE side."

*[Pete introduces himself, then Mark. Then the SPORTFIVE side:]*

> "Great, thank you both. And from SPORTFIVE: I'm Rahul, I look after brand partnerships in our Formula 1 business. Bjorn, Knut, over to you for a quick hello."

*[Bjorn and Knut say hello, two or three sentences each. Then hand the floor to the brand:]*

> "Perfect. Pete, maybe the best place to start is if you tell Mark a bit about CodeRabbit and what you're looking to achieve, and we'll take it from there."

---

## After the call (checklist)

- Thank-you email the same day to Pete and Mark: three lines, the agreed next steps and owners.
- Note what Haas offered and what CodeRabbit reacted to, and feed it into the brief.
- Log the call in the register.
