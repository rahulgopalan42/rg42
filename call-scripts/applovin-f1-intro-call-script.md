## Boilerplate: what AppLovin does

- **About AppLovin:** AppLovin (Nasdaq: APP) is a Palo Alto advertising technology company that helps businesses find customers through ads shown inside mobile games and apps, and more and more for brands that sell on the web. Its main product, AppLovin Ads, is driven by Axon, an AI engine that decides which ad to show to whom, and advertisers buy on results such as sales, new customers or leads. It also owns MAX, which helps app developers sell their ad space, Adjust for measurement, and Wurl for connected TV. Having grown up in mobile games, it sold its own games studios to Tripledot in June 2025 to focus on advertising, and it has been bringing in e-commerce and other web advertisers since 2024. It's one of the most profitable companies in tech: 2025 revenue was $5.48 billion, up 70%, with net income of $3.33 billion and fewer than 900 full-time staff, and it joined the S&P 500 in September 2025. In June 2026 it opened AppLovin Ads to any business, self-serve, saying it reaches more than a billion people a day, and it now calls itself "a leading marketing platform" for businesses of every size.
- **In one line, if someone asks on the call:** "They're an ad company. Their AI picks which ad to show inside mobile games and apps, and advertisers buy on results, like sales or sign-ups. They grew up in gaming, and now they're opening it up to every kind of business."

## 4. Questions to ask them

*[A conversation, not a form. Ask one, then stop and listen.]*

**1. Priorities**

> "You opened AppLovin Ads up to everyone in June. Over the next year, how much is still about games, where you're strongest, and how much is e-commerce and the newer categories?"

**2. Markets**

> "About half your revenue already comes from outside the US. Where do you most want to grow the advertiser base next? Europe, Asia, Latin America?"

- *Follow-up:* "Is there anywhere you feel less known than you should be?"

**3. Buyers and decision makers**

> "Who's the buyer you most want to win right now? Heads of growth at mid-sized brands? Performance agencies? Who signs off on a new platform?"

- *Follow-up:* "If I gave you a paddock weekend tomorrow, who would you bring?"
- *If they name growth and marketing leaders:* "That's exactly the kind of list a hosting program gets built around."

**4. Their own brand**

> "Your whole pitch is performance, every advertiser buys on results. So how do you think about your own brand? Is the rename to AppLovin Ads the start of putting more behind it?"

- *Follow-up:* "Have you done anything in sport before? And is there anything you'd never do?"

**5. B2B, and the other audiences**

- *Follow-up:* "How hard is it to hire the AI people you want right now?"

> "If you did something like this, how would it get judged internally, and by whom? I'm guessing you'd want it measured like an ad campaign."
