## Boilerplate: what AssemblyAI does

- **About AssemblyAI:** AssemblyAI is a speech AI company that builds and trains its own models for turning audio into text and meaning, and sells them to developers through an API. Companies use it inside products such as AI meeting notes, contact-centre analytics, medical note-taking and media transcription, and AssemblyAI says more than 5,000 companies now build on it. It was founded in 2017 by CEO Dylan Fox, came through Y Combinator, and has raised about $115M, most recently a $50M Series C led by Accel in December 2023, with Insight Partners among its backers. It has offices in San Francisco and New York and a remote-first team across 16 countries, it says. In 2026 it has moved from transcription towards the whole voice layer, launching a speech model you can instruct in plain language, a Voice Agent API that listens, reasons and talks back over one connection, and a new real-time model trained for voice agents.
- **In one line, if someone asks on the call:** "They make the AI that listens. It turns what people say in calls, meetings and videos into accurate text, and now it powers voice assistants that talk back."
