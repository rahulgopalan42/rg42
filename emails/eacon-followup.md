As you're new to Formula 1, here's a quick view of how it could support EACON as you grow internationally:

- New markets: F1 races in markets you're expanding into, including Australia, Canada and the Middle East, as well as Shanghai at home. That gives you a reason to host customers in each market every year, and helps build your name quickly where buyers don't know you yet.
- Your buyers: the decision to automate a mine sits with a small group of senior people. F1 has the highest concentration of business decision makers of any major sport, and a race weekend gives you three days with them.
- Talent: F1 is a strong draw for engineers, which is why Komatsu and Ma'aden both use it to reach future talent. That matters for a company hiring engineers across several countries.
- A shared story: the 2026 rules made the cars far more electric, so managing energy under hard limits is now central to winning. That's a natural story for a business running a large and growing electric fleet.
