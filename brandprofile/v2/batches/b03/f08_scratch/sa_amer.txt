Americanas Company Description 
Americanas S.A. operates in the e-commerce business in Brazil.
It offers e-commerce digital platforms for various digital solutions. The company operates americanas.com, an online store with various products; Ame, a platform for financial and non-financial products and services; and Puket, an online apparel platform; MinD, an online design and home decoration platform.
In addition, it operates physical stores and multi-brand e-commerce channels under the Imaginarium brand; and a retail chain of fruits, vegetables, and greens under Hortifruti Natural da Terra brand, as well as provides Lovebrands, an online platform that offers various products and services; Skoob, a virtual library platform.
Americanas S.A. was founded in 1999 and is headquartered in Rio De Janeiro, Brazil.
