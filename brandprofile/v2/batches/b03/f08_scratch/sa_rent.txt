Localiza Rent a Car Company Description 
Localiza Rent a Car S.A., together with its subsidiaries, engages in the car rental business in Brazil and internationally.
It is involved in fleet administration and management; sale and support of used cars; granting franchises; vehicle claims management; provision of tracking and telemetry solutions; travel and tourism agency services; and other automotive services.
The company also offers Zarp, an app-based network dedicated to drivers for car rental. It serves individual and legal entities.
The company was founded in 1973 and is headquartered in Belo Horizonte, Brazil.
