Anker, Seek Ultimate
 Anker is a global smart hardware technology company driven by ultimate innovation. Since its foundation in 2011, Anker has innovated across smart hardware, software, and AI — delivering products spanning charging, energy, smart home, audio and video, and creative tools under the Anker brand, and serving 200M+ consumers in 180+ countries and regions.
One Anker, One Brand
As AI and connected devices bring once-standalone products into one intelligent ecosystem, we're bringing it all together under one name: Anker.
Anker Charging: Mobile Charging and Accessories • Anker SOLIX: Home and Portable Energy • Anker eufy: Smart Home • Anker soundcore: Audio and Video • Anker eufyMake: Creative Tools
Five product categories. One unified brand.
Today, Anker is building a connected platform guided by our commitment to Ultimate Innovation, bringing our technologies, products, and experiences together under a clearer promise to the world.
Anker. Where Ultimate Meets Possible.
The Anker eufy Brand Story: AI-Powered Living, Built with Care
Life is made up of moments—big and small, seen and unseen. At Anker eufy, we believe in building intelligent technology that enhances every aspect of your life, making each day cleaner, safer, and more fulfilling.
At the heart of every aspect of Anker eufy is Caring Intelligence™, AI that anticipates, protects, and empowers your daily life.
Anker eufy Clean: Delivers a cleaner home with AI-driven deep-cleaning robotic vacuums and robotic lawn mowers that adapt to your family's smart home needs.
Anker eufy Security: Powers a safer home using local AI-enhanced security systems to learn and respond to potential risks before they happen.
Anker eufy Baby: eufy's feeding ecosystem. Whether At home, at work, or on the go-stay ready, fresh, and present for your baby.
Anker eufyMake: Inspires a more creative home, enriched by AI-powered UV printing, transforming ideas into tangible designs.
Whether it's cleaning, security, or creativity, Anker eufy's AI-driven solutions transform every home into a smart, caring, and inspiring space. Because care isn't just a feature—it's a way of living.
Anker eufy—AI-Powered Living, Built with Care
