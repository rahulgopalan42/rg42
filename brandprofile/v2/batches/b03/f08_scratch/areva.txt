 10 years of Flexity Vienna. 🚋 
What started as a city project is now a global success story. 
For a decade, the City of Vienna, Wiener Linien and Alstom have worked together to shape the future of urban mobility, combining trust, expertise and real-life experience from daily tram operations.
Today, Vienna is Alstom’s global centre of excellence for trams and light rail. The expertise built here is helping cities around the world move forward, from Vienna all the way to Melbourne.

 As European locomotive operations become increasingly cross-border, maintenance support needs to travel with them 🧰
With more than 2,500 #Traxx locomotives in service across Europe, supporting the fleet means mobilising expertise and resources wherever they operate.
That’s where the Traxx Maintenance Performance Centre comes in. #AlstomServices brings together maintenance specialists across Europe, combining the strength of a wider network with support close to customers and their fleets.
