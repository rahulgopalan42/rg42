KEY JUDGEMENT: K-OTC COMPANIES
- Many large unlisted Korean companies are "designated" on K-OTC, the over-the-counter market run by KOFIA, where shares can trade
  although the company is not exchange-listed. Because the eligibility rule is "own shares not publicly traded", every such company
  is held in WATCH with an "eligibility_flag" (route met): HD Hyundai Oilbank, Hyundai Card, Kakao Mobility, POSCO E&C, SK ecoplant,
  Hyundai Engineering, Lotte E&C, LS Cable & System, SEMES, Samsung Medison, Korea South-East Power. The user should rule on these.
  If K-OTC companies count as non-public, ten of them move to MAIN (Kakao Mobility has no route proven).
