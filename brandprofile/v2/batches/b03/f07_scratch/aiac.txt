Industrial Investment and Management
American Industrial Acquisition Corporation and its subsidiaries and affiliates (“AIAC”) is a privately held industrial investment portfolio with a long term mission to build enduring businesses
The AIAC portfolio consists of 122 administrative, manufacturing and distribution sites in 24 countries in North America, Europe, Asia, Africa and Australia. We own over 6.5 million square feet of industrial real estate and hold the exclusive license to responsibly manage 22 million acres of prime Canadian timberland, a land mass in excess of the size of the nation of Austria.
AIAC, through its affiliates, has purchased and operated manufacturing and distribution units of Ahlstrom, Allegion, Astellas, Blackstone, Boeing, Carlyle Group, Constellium, GlaxoSmithKline, Ingersoll Rand, Jabil Circuit, Johnson Controls, KKR, Kodak, Lockheed Martin, Merck, Moog, Northrop Grumman, Novelis, Pfizer, Raytheon Technologies, Rexel Group, The Riverside Company, Sandvik, Senior, Siemens, Smiths, Sonepar, SSC, Suez Group, Tolko, Vallourec, Visteon and other large multinational corporations. In addition to purchasing units of large multinational public companies, AIAC affiliates purchase the equity and debt of privately held companies, and own and control a global intellectual property portfolio of patents, brand names, and trademarks.
