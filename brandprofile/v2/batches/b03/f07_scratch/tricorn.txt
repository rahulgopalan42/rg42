Design & Engineering
You need an effective package design that stands out in the market, delights customers, and drives brand loyalty. Custom packaging can achieve all of this and more. TricorBraun’s Supply Chain and award-winning Design & Engineering teams work with you to streamline your supply chain, improve your margins, and achieve your sustainability goals with a custom package that helps your business grow.

Sourcing
Find the right packaging for your product and your business. From stock to custom, access options across all major materials, with guidance built around your brand, budget, and supply chain needs. Our global supplier network gives you flexibility and choice, with one partner to help bring it all together.

Logistics
Supply chain logistics are complicated and time-consuming—we can give you peace of mind. We save you time and address the complexity by managing your inventory, reducing your costs, ensuring the quality of your packaging, and efficiently replenishing your supply. Your packaging needs are complex. At TricorBraun, we manage everything from your global packaging logistics to local warehousing so you can focus on growing your business.

Sustainability
Sustainability has become a business imperative—and, at TricorBraun, we are committed to sustainable and ethical practices because it’s good for our customers, our business, and the planet. From our Supplier Code of Conduct to the release of our Sustainability Report, we walk the walk and help our customers by delivering top-quality, safe, sustainable packaging formats that protect and preserve your product. 
Learn More
In-House Mold Development
TricorBraun offers in-house custom molded plastic container development and a production tooling shop. These capabilities give customers the flexibility and speed to market they’re looking for. Our team has the technical expertise to provide commercial, prototype, and repair services for extrusion blow molding (EBM) and single and two-stage PET cavities for injection stretch blow molding.

Why TricorBraun
Your packaging requirements are complex. TricorBraun makes choosing, sourcing, and designing packaging simple for your business. Whether you need stock solutions, a custom design, rigid/flexible packaging, or sustainable options, we tailor a packaging program to match your needs and take the stress out of the process. We’re here to help you win in the marketplace. 
Learn More
Our Story
TricorBraun has been a leader in the packaging industry for 120 years. We bring over a century of expertise, talent, and resources to developing packaging solutions to help our customers win. Since our early days, we’ve supplied customers with glass bottles and other packaging through major economic and societal changes: the Great Depression, two World Wars, recessions, the 2008 financial crisis, and now most recently the COVID pandemic—all while growing to be a worldwide leader in sourcing and designing packaging. 
Learn More
Awards
TricorBraun is honored to be recognized by some of the most prestigious organizations in the industry for our innovative designs and our commitment to creativity, functionality, consumer experience, and sustainability.

Our Culture
TricorBraun has been a leader in the packaging industry for over a century. While much has changed over the last 120 years, our core values and principles remain the root of our success and guide us in everything we do, every day. Doing the right thing is in our DNA. It’s why we’re a valued partner to suppliers around the globe, and a trusted advisor of customers ranging from cutting edge start-ups to the world’s most iconic brands, served by the best team of packaging professionals in the world. 
Learn More
Leadership
With expert insight, experience, vision, commitment and integrity, TricorBraun’s Leadership Team is dedicated to furthering the success of our team members, customers, suppliers and stakeholders. With a combined tenure in the industry of almost a century, together we set the standard for excellence in the marketplace around the world.

Locations
TricorBraun operates over 100 locations across the Americas, Europe, Asia, and Australia. This extensive network enables us to provide high-quality packaging solutions and competitive pricing to a diverse range of industries, including food and beverage, healthcare, personal care, and industrial sectors. Our strategic locations ensure efficient distribution and exceptional customer service worldwide. Our commitment to innovation and excellence has allowed us to become a trusted partner in the packaging industry.
