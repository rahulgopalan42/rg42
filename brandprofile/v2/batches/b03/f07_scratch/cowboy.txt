Big Iron
Built for Orbit
Big Iron is designed to carry its data center into orbit. There, the outer shell will open into radiators and solar wings will deploy to power four compute racks.
Big Iron is designed to stand 90 meters tall, with nine Clydesdale engines on the booster and one on the upper stage.
