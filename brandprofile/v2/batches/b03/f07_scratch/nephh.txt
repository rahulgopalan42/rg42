Our history
 From its early role in insurance-linked investing to the development of a broader, integrated risk platform, Nephila's history reflects a consistent ability to adapt, evolve and take a long-term view.
 1997
Foundations
 Founded by Frank Majors and Greg Hagood within Willis Ltd., Nephila was built
 on a simple but powerful insight: catastrophe risk could be analyzed, priced and
 invested in with the same rigor as traditional financial assets. From the outset,
 the firm focused on insurance-linked securities tied to natural catastrophes.
 This asset class was largely unexplored, but rich with opportunity for those willing
 to approach it scientifically.
 2005
Broadening the opportunity set
 By 2005, Nephila had begun expanding what insurance-linked investing could
 address. The firm launched the first dedicated weather derivative strategy,
 applying insurance and capital markets expertise to weather-driven exposures.
 That early work has since evolved into Nephila Climate, today focused on risk
 transfer solutions across renewable energy, storage, agriculture resilience and
 other climate-linked exposures.
 2012
Scaling the platform
 Through the following decade, the platform deepened and expanded. Around
 2012, Nephila was firmly establishing itself as a leading institutional manager in
 the ILS space by scaling assets, refining its analytical capabilities and broadening
 its participation. This period represents the firm’s transition from pioneer to market
 leader, with growing institutional adoption and a more diversified investment
 platform.
Deepening market access
 This evolution accelerated in the mid-2010s with the launch of Velocity Risk
 Underwriters, representing a strategic move upstream into primary insurance
 origination. Nephila enhanced its underwriting insight, improved data access
 and strengthened its ability to proactively source and shape opportunities rather
 than relying solely on secondary market participation.
 2018
Strengthening the platform
 A defining transformation came in 2018, when Nephila became part of Markel
 Group. On November 14, 2018, Markel acquired 100% of the equity interest in
 Nephila Holdings Ltd. from KKR and MAN Group. This partnership combined
 Nephila’s investment expertise with a permanent balance sheet, unlocking new
 capabilities in capital efficiency, portfolio construction and liquidity, while
 preserving the firm’s independence in investment decision-making.
 2022
Expanding into specialty
 In 2022, Lloyd’s Syndicate 2358 began underwriting as Nephila’s Specialty Risk
 strategy at Lloyd’s, focused on non-catastrophe lines such as energy, aviation,
 cyber and other specialty exposures. This addition to the platform demonstrates
 Nephila’s ability to source, structure and optimize risk across the full insurance
 value chain.
 2026
Looking forward
 Today, Nephila operates at the forefront of innovation in insurance-linked
 investing, continuously evolving how risk is understood, priced and transferred.
 Building on decades of experience, the firm integrates advanced analytics,
 climate science and real-time market intelligence to shape portfolios that
 anticipate global risk dynamics.
