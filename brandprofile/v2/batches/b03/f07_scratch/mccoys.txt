About McCoy's Building Supply 
McCoy’s currently operates 85 retail building supply stores with full-service lumberyards in three states: Texas, New Mexico, and Oklahoma. Our customer base includes do-it-yourselfers of all skill levels, farmers and ranchers, repair and remodel contractors, and independent homebuilders.
Our Purpose
To make life easier and more fulfilling for those who build.
Our Values
Professional Excellence
The standards for leadership at McCoy’s are unapologetically high. We are students of our jobs, our company, our industry, and our communities.
Hard Work
Our jobs are intense and difficult at times. We ask for help when we need to and grow with grace.
Integrity
We always aim to be an example of the behavior we want to see in others.
Hospitality
We want to learn the stories of our team members and our customer’s, welcoming them into McCoy’s Born to Build® atmosphere.
Service to Others
We elevate our communities through our commitment to giving back whether with our time, our talents, or donations. 
Ride for the Brand
We protect the McCoy’s brand by making sure our stores are a place where folks greet you by name and ask what you’re working on. We lead while keeping a legacy in mind for our customers and communities.

Always Closed Sundays
Even in today's fiercely competitive market, McCoy's adheres to a long-standing decision to remain closed on Sundays. Our employees appreciate being part of a company that allows them a dependable day off each week. Additionally, you'll find our employees enjoying company-wide holidays on New Year's Day, Independence Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, and Christmas Day. You can place orders on our website any time you like and pick them up during our normal business hours Monday through Saturday or schedule a delivery.
