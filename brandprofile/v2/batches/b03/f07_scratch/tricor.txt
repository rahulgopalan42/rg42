Why TricorBraun
Your packaging requirements are complex. TricorBraun makes choosing, sourcing, and designing packaging simple for your business. Whether you need stock solutions, a custom design, rigid/flexible packaging, or sustainable options, we tailor a packaging program to match your needs and take the stress out of the process. We’re here to help you win in the marketplace. 
Learn More
Our Story
TricorBraun has been a leader in the packaging industry for 120 years. We bring over a century of expertise, talent, and resources to developing packaging solutions to help our customers win. Since our early days, we’ve supplied customers with glass bottles and other packaging through major economic and societal changes: the Great Depression, two World Wars, recessions, the 2008 financial crisis, and now most recently the COVID pandemic—all while growing to be a worldwide leader in sourcing and designing packaging.

Passion for Packaging. Built Over 120 Years.
Packaging is often the first physical interaction a consumer has with your brand. It must capture attention, communicate value, and perform flawlessly. At the same time, it must run efficiently on filling lines, arrive on schedule, meet cost targets, and support sustainability expectations. 
That balance is what we’ve helped brands navigate for more than 120 years. 
TricorBraun began as a family-run business with a simple belief: packaging should not be the hardest part of bringing a product to market. Over generations, we’ve grown alongside our customers, expanding from distribution into global sourcing, custom design, quality oversight, and supply chain expertise. 
Markets evolve. Materials change. Consumer expectations rise. Our role remains consistent: helping brands make smart packaging decisions with clarity and confidence. 
Here’s how we bring that to life for our customers: 
Procurement: Global sourcing across major substrates with options aligned to brand, budget, and performance goals. 
Design & Engineering: End-to-end custom packaging development grounded in manufacturability and market insight. 
Logistics & Warehousing: Global supply coordination, regional warehousing, and inventory programs aligned to real production schedules. 
Quality & Technical Support: Testing, validation, supplier oversight, and proactive issue prevention to support smooth commercialization. 
Sustainability: Material innovation, footprint reduction strategies, and design approaches that support circularity and regulatory evolution. 
Dedicated Partnership: Hands-on guidance across every stage, with teams empowered to solve complex challenges quickly and practically.

Where is TricorBraun headquartered?
