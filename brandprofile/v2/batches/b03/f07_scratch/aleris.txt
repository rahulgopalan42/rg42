Shaping a Sustainable World Together 
Novelis partners with customers across all industries to deliver innovative, high‑performance aluminum solutions that enable lighter, stronger, and more sustainable products. Our operations are built around a circular business model, maximizing the use of recycled aluminum while continuously reducing carbon emissions across our value chain. Through global scale, advanced rolling and recycling capabilities, and deep technical expertise, we turn aluminum into a material that meets the demands of today and tomorrow. Guided by our purpose, we work every day to shape a more sustainable aluminum industry – together with our customers and partners.
