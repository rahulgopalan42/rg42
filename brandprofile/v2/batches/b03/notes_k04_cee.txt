k04_cee (batch 3, MODE country: CEE, Greece, Ukraine). Researcher notes, 28 Nov 2026.

SOURCES USED (names extracted)
- Coface CEE Top 500 Companies 2025 (17th edition, Nov 2025), PDF hosted at hrvatski-izvoznici.hr. Covers PL, CZ, HU, RO, SK, BG, HR, SI, RS, LT, LV, EE.
  Turnover 2023/2024 (EUR m), net profit and employees per company. 494 of 500 rows parsed with pdfplumber, plus ranks 2 and 4 added by hand.
  Some names came out garbled because the layout is multi-line; most were fixed by hand from the text layout (scratch_k04/fix.py), and about 6 remain "(unparsed name)".
  Lowest entry EUR 708m, so every eligible company on the list meets Route A on revenue. Note: an asterisk in Coface means "consolidated", not listed.
- en.wikipedia "List of companies of Greece" (first 100k characters only, about 210 names) and "List of companies of Ukraine" (84 names). These pages carry no figures.
- Company pages, plus cs/pl/hu/hr/en Wikipedia articles, for the research.
- Prague Stock Exchange share lists (prime, standard, free, start) and the Nasdaq Baltic share list, read 28 Nov 2026 to check listing status.
- NOT reached: Czech Top 100, HVG/Figyelő Top 200, Rzeczpospolita Lista 500, TREND Top/Finstat (Cloudflare blocked them), Romanian, Bulgarian, Serbian and Croatian national lists,
  Forbes Ukraine lists, and the Greek ICAP rankings. The Warsaw, Budapest, Bucharest, Zagreb, Ljubljana, Sofia and Belgrade exchange lists did not load in a usable form.
- WebSearch used: 1 of 2 (it found the Coface PDF). Wikipedia API rate-limited most bulk requests, so pages were opened one at a time through WebFetch.
