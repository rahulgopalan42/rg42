About Us

Al Ahram Taqa FZCO is a member of Jaied MHA Holding FZCO for Trading and Industries, which was established more than 38 years ago. Al Ahram Taqa FZCO provides a full range of power solutions to address both temporary and long-term power demands, including rental power projects ranging from 1 MW to 1000 MW within the Middle East and worldwide. With an experienced technical team skilled in meeting energy needs, we offer comprehensive solutions tailored to our customers’ requirements.

Our services include:

- Supply, Implementation, Installation and Commissioning of Power Plants up to 1000MW

- Supply, Implementation, Installation and Commissioning of Power Plants up to 765KV

- Supply, Implementation, Installation and Commissioning of Transmision Lines up to 400KV

- Overhauling & Maintenance of Power Stations

- Construction of Highways, Roads and Soil Refilling Works

- Construction, Erection and Installation of Power Plants

- Supply and Erection of Fuel Tanks TANKS

- Supply OF Spare Parts for Power Stations

- Operation and Maintenance works for Power Stations

Our head office is located in Jebel Ali Free Zone, Dubai, United Arab Emirates. We also have regional offices in Yemen, Egypt, and Oman, and partners in Saudi Arabia and the African continent. We are committed to meeting our customers' energy needs through continuous improvement processes (CIP), enhancing the performance of our engines and auxiliary equipment, including high-speed, medium/low-speed, HFO, LFO, and gas power plants. The company has played a vital role in supporting urban and rural electrification projects, as well as water treatment plants that supply treated water to rural areas.

Al Ahram Taqa FZCO is a leading company in executing turnkey power projects and rental power projects, serving as an EPC contractor and main supplier. Our projects include power plants (CPP and base load), high and medium voltage substations, and transmission lines (11 kV, 33 kV, 132 kV, 220 kV, 400 kV), as well as civil and electro-mechanical erection works. We have contributed over 50% of the total power generated into Yemen’s national grid. Since our founding, we have undertaken numerous major power projects and continue to expand our footprint in the power sector.

Through sincere and committed business relationships, we provide the best offers and technical support focused on customer requirements. We have built an excellent rapport with many international companies and governmental institutions. We have established and commissioned power plants with a total output of 800 MW (turnkey and rental power projects), in addition to 200 MW of DDP-supplied power plants, and recently awarded rental power projects totaling 141 MW. We also have 250 MW of power projects in the pipeline. Since our establishment, we have prioritized delivering quality products and services, focusing on customer needs to build long-lasting relationships. Our goal is to develop business relations with customers and multinational companies, and we are dedicated to maintaining and enhancing our business activities in the region through continuous improvement in products, spare parts, and services. Al Ahram Taqa FZCO has been awarded ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 14001:2015 certifications for quality management by AQSR, and we are committed to expanding our global presence by adhering to the highest quality management standards.

Our Vision

Al Ahram Taqa FZCO is a member of Jaied MHA Holding FZCO, which is founded on a solid foundation of success and accumulated experience. The company has established itself as a leader in the power generation sector, distinguishing itself by executing some of the largest power generation projects in Yemen. We are committed to optimizing equipment performance while adhering to global quality and environmental standards. Our success is driven by supplying high-quality equipment from internationally reputable manufacturers and providing exceptional after-sales service.


Al Ahram Taqa FZCO contributes to national development by creating new job opportunities for youth in Yemen's power sector. We adhere to a consistent policy of selecting the right employees, enhancing their careers, and developing their technical skills through collaboration with experts from reputable multinational companies. This approach helps us maintain a high level of trust and respect with our valued customers.
