mateco

Access technology from experienced pros

mateco is one of the leading suppliers for renting working platforms. Our fleet of rental equipment currently comprises over 13,000 working platforms in more than 700 different designs. With 185 of our own locations in Europe, Panama, Mexico and Chile and more than 80 strong partners around the world, we pursue a clear goal: to supply the best height access technology for our customers. That has made us the industry professionals for more than 50 years now.

In the process, we've always been committed to the topic of safety. We therefore not only focus on the latest equipment technology, a constant certification and qualified personnel. We also offer training for the operators of working platforms – the PlatformCard.

Our rental fleet consists of:

  • Truck- and trailer-mounted working platforms
  • Scissors and telescopic working platforms
  • Special working platforms
  • Telescopic forklifts
  • Articulating booms
  • Low level access working platforms

With them we realise working heights of up to 90 m, lateral reaches of up to 43 m and load capacities of up to 7,200 kg. And always with the goal of supplying the best height access technology for your application.

In addition to our nationwide network of branches close to our customers, all the threads come together at our modern company headquarters in Leonberg. This is where all the central corporate divisions are controlled, which are divided into the following departments, among others: Accounting, Equipment Management, Insurance, Marketing and Communications, Human Resources and Social Affairs, and IT.
