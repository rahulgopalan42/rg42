Machines in the fleet

We are the leading provider of sales and rental—specialized in aerial working platforms, forklifts and associated services. We are proud to offer the largest working platform rental fleet of approx. 45.000 units in more than 185 locations in Europe, Panama, Mexico and Chile.

Having an area-wide network of depots all over Europe and an increasing network in South America is our secret of success.

Safe, fast, close and reliable

We have specialized logistics and special equipment all over the world.

With over 80 international partners, we also rent equipment outside our own companies in Germany, Switzerland, Luxembourg, Poland, Belgium, the Netherlands, Spain, the Czech Republic, Slovakia, Hungary, Romania, Bulgaria, Panama, Mexico and Chile and offer a suitable solution for your access problem worldwide. The entire process (quotation, ordering, invoicing, etc.) is handled by our International Division in Leonberg. The rented access platforms as well as the technical service are then provided by our local partner in the usual mateco quality.

If you would like to rent working platforms worldwide, please contact our International Division at International@mateco.de.

In addition, our Special Equipment Division specializes in niche machines that are usually not available locally in the respective markets. For more information on Special Equipment, please click here.
