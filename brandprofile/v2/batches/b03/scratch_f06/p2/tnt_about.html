Years of Experience

In 1985, our journey began in Houston, Texas with a single 18 Ton crane and a straightforward philosophy: provide our customers with outstanding service and ensure a safe and enduring career for our employees.

Over the years, TNT Crane & Rigging has grown exponentially, evolving into one of the largest crane companies in North America. In 2007, we expanded to three branches with a fleet of 55 cranes, 150 employees, and serving approximately 1,000 customers. By 2013, our fleet had grown to an impressive 439 cranes, operating from 21 branches.

Today, we stand proud with a network of over 40 branches, a fleet comprising 700 cranes, a team of more than 1,700 dedicated employees, and the trust of more than 4,000 customers. The acquisitions of successful companies such as Southway Crane & Rigging, RMS Cranes, Eagle West Cranes, Stampede Crane & Rigging, and JMS Crane & Rigging has granted our company with the ability to provide comprehensive lifting solutions to our customers across North America.

Our success remains rooted in our steadfast dedication to delivering unparalleled service, providing reliable and modern equipment, and prioritizing the safety and well-being of our team.

We take pride in our rich history, our enduring commitment to excellence, and our vision for a future where we consistently work to set the benchmark in the crane and rigging industry.

A Culture of Care & Concern

When we say our employees are our most valuable resource, we truly mean it. This belief is deeply ingrained in our ethos and guides our decision-making processes at every level of the company. Our unwavering commitment to creating an Incident and Injury-Free (IIF) workplace is a testament to this belief. We invest significant resources in training and continuous process improvements to ensure that every employee arrives at work and returns home safely, each and every day.

Training & Certification

All TNT operators have proven their competency through our operator evaluation program and hold NCCCO & NCCER Certifications.

TNT Crane & Rigging strives to stay at the front of this training and certification with both in-house, hands-on training as well as industry specific 3rd party training and certification.
