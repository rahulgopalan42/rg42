About Us

McNaughton-McKay Electric Company is a wholesale electrical distributor serving markets in Michigan, Ohio, Georgia, North Carolina and South Carolina. We offer a full line of products ranging from pipe and wire to complex automation control systems for everyone from small electrical contractors to large-scale manufacturers. McNaughton-McKay Electric Company is a member of McNaughton McKay Group (MMG). MMG has more than 60 locations covering 9 states and ~1,900 empowered owners.

In addition to our core product offering, we provide services and solutions to support your applications through innovative capabilities, processes, technologies and personnel you can depend on to move your business forward. Our expert sales and engineering teams are here to help solve your problems and will go above and beyond to understand your business and meet your needs. As a 100% employee-owned company, our teams are personally invested in your success.

Our relationships aren't just about selling products—we focus on creating long-term value that contributes to the success and profitability of our customers. 

Who We Are:

More than an electrical distributor.

|Leadership

From the early days of John McNaughton and Arch McKay, we’ve evolved to a team of empowered owners dedicated to serving the unique customers and suppliers across our various regions. Our leadership team is made up of individuals who share our vision for business and have the skills necessary to empower their fellow employee-owners to drive customer service, meet challenges head-on and continually exceed expectations. It is this impassioned approach that enables us, as a company, to continue as a leader in the electrical distribution industry.

|Why McNaughton-McKay?

While markets, technologies and processes evolve, McNaughton-McKay's core principles of selling quality products, maintaining large and diversified inventories and ensuring on-time deliveries will never change. The breadth and depth of our distribution capabilities, coupled with our considerable inventory and over one million square feet of warehouse space, positions us to be your trusted partner, giving you access to best-in-class products, innovative industry solutions and superior customer service so you can focus on your business goals.

For more than 100 years, we have led the way in the distribution of electrical and automation products for industrial and commercial markets. Our comprehensive solutions help manufacturers eliminate risk and waste while improving productivity and cycle time. We specialize in improving quality and compliance, increasing yields and automating complex processes. 

Our multitude of products and services help commercial construction customers save time, resources and overall job costs. Finally, we work to be good stewards of our environment with our renewable energy products and solutions, providing the highest quality energy efficient products for solar energy new construction and upgrade projects.
