A Sammons Enterprises Company

Sammons Enterprises, Inc., is a diverse holding company composed of financial services, industrial equipment, real estate investment, and infrastructure businesses operating in four countries. Headquartered in Dallas, Texas (US), Sammons was established in 1938. With over $7 billion in annual revenues and over $131 billion in assets, Sammons is one of the largest privately held companies in the US.
