Companies using FareHarbor

FareHarbor is the leading booking and business management solution for tours, activities, rentals and attractions.

Over a decade ago, our founders wanted to reserve surfboards online in Hawaii. Tourism businesses rarely took bookings online and when they did, the systems were unreliable. So our founders set out to change this…

Today, we are the #1 booking software in the market powering billions of experiences for 23,000+ businesses across the globe. We are continuously working to be the leading partner to experience providers in a changing digital world.

Global reach
