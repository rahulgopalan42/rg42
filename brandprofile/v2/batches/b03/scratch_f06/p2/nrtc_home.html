    Military Radios

    V/UHF Software Defined Radios (SDR)

    NRTC V/UHF radio family is designed and developed using frequency-agile, low-power SDR hardware with latest fast-processing FPGAs. Its waveforms are designed for robust performance in the most demanding dynamic environments.


    EOD & Robotics

    Hadaf EOD Robots

    NRTC Tracked RCV (H IM & H IM Alpha)

    NRTC EOD TRACKED RCV is highly ruggedized, reliable and light-weight vehicle, best suitable for Counter IED and Reconnaissance Operations

    Features provided by Hadaf EOD Robots are: Ruggedized Tracked Platform, Stair climbing capability 45D, Equipped with 4-DOF Robotic arm, Manipulate up to 40 lbs. of IED, On-board 4x Night vision cameras for live feedback, Dual Speed Modes for Precise Accuracy, Portable Ground Control Station, and User-Friendly Interface.


    Security Surveillance & Smart City

    Self-sustained Ground Surveillance System

    A self-sustained Ground Surveillance system provides an early warning of a hostile approach of personnel or vehicle toward a protected area. The system creates a virtual fence using PIR sensors, X-band sensors, and seismic sensors.PIR, X-band, and seismic sensors are installed at various locations around the Aol. In an event of a possible intrusion or trespassing, an alarm is generated. Any approach towards this virtual perimeter is detected and transmitted to a monitoring station and the intrusion is localized.
