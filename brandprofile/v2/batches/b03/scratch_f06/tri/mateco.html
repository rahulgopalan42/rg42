Rent aerial work platforms nationwide with mateco

Aerial platforms are rented
from the professional.

Our rental fleet comprises over 13,000 aerial platforms in more than 700 different models. With a network of over 185 own locations in Europe, Panama, Mexico, and Chile and more than 80 strong partners worldwide, it is our goal to provide you with the best access technology available.

Our working platforms can reach heights up to 90 m, lateral ranges up to 43 m, and payloads up to 7,200 kg. No matter what height problem you want to solve, we have the right solution for you. Whether truck-mounted aerial platforms, trailer-mounted aerial platforms, scissor working platform, telescopic working platform, articulating-boom working platform or rolling lift – at mateco, you will find the right aerial platform for your project.

Our wide-ranging offer makes us the first point of contact for all types of aerial work. Our experienced consultants will help you choose the right working platform to complete your project safely and efficiently.

If you have any questions about our mateco aerial platforms, do not hesitate to contact us. Whether by phone, by email or through our website - we are here for you.

Trainings

For safe and efficient
working with working platforms

With mateco working platforms, you can easily overcome great heights without compromising on safety. The safety of man and machine is our top priority. That's why we not only invest in state-of-the-art equipment technology, but also ensure that our safety standards are constantly certified and that our personnel are highly qualified.

To ensure the safe operation of our working plattforms, forklift trucks and telescopic forklifts we offer comprehensive training courses that impart both theoretical knowledge and practical experience. The training courses teach you and your team how to operate and maintain the equipment safely and efficiently. Our experienced trainers will show you the best practices and help you understand all safety aspects.

In addition, we offer annual training to ensure that your employees are always up to date. Whether it's the correct handling of equipment, compliance with safety regulations or solving specific problems - with mateco you are well equipped. Rely on our experience and expertise to make your project a safe and efficient success.
