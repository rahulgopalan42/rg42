Mission

To produce oil and gas while also involving ourselves strategically with other links in the chain, in an efficient and safe manner and with financial discipline, generating value for our shareholders and society.

Purpose

To maximize the value of our oil and gas production, applying responsibility and discipline, while contributing to energy security.

Vision

We are guided by non-negotiable principles that drive us to generate value for both shareholders and society.

TOGETHER WE ARE STRONGER

SAFETY ALWAYS FIRST

A non-negotiable basis for all our operations.

RESPONSIBLE BUSINESS PRACTICES

Our team is highly trained and delivers results.

Agility, cost discipline and efficiency in our activities.

Strategic Fronts

For everything to come to fruition, we act on different strategic fronts:

  • Short Term

    Focus on production assets.

  • Medium Term

    Acquisition and revitalization of new assets.

  • Long Term

    Development of exploration assets selectively

UPSTREAMExploration and Production

Applying technology and responsibility, we operate in the first stage of the oil and natural gas supply chain:
  • Efficient production of oil and natural gas
  • Development of exploratory assets, selectively and a low risk
  • Maximizing value with financial discipline
  • Effective contribution to the country's energy security

Meet the members

Richard Kovacs

Chief Executive Officer

Mr. Richard Kehrer Kovacs holds a degree in Economics from PUC-SP and has extensive experience in the financial and energy sectors, holding positions in leadership, commercial structuring and new business development. He is currently CEO of Ebrasil LNG, Commercial Director of Ebrasil Energia and member of the Board of Directors of Ocyan. From August 2025 to January 2026, before becoming CEO of BRAVA, Mr. Richard was Chairman of the Company’s Board of Directors.

Next

Luiz Carvalho

Chief Financial and Investor Relations Officer

Mr. Luiz Carvalho holds a Production Engineering degree from the State University of Rio de Janeiro (UERJ) and has executive education courses at Harvard and Wharton. He led the Oil, Gas and Petrochemicals coverage in Latin America for 13 years, with previous positions at BTG Pactual, UBS and HSBC. With experience in the sector since 2004, he was recognized for several consecutive years as the #1 Oil and Gas analyst by Institutional Investor, both in Brazil and Latin America. Before entering the financial market, he gained experience in the energy industry, with positions at Shell and Transocean, where he worked in the sectors of fuel distribution and exploration & production.

Previous
Next

Carlos Travassos

Chief Offshore Operational Officer

Mr. Travassos holds a degree in Mechanical Engineering and a postgraduate degree in Business Management in Oil & Gas and Renewable Energy. Mr. Travassos has 39 years of experience and held the position of Vice President of Investment and Digital Technologies at Braskem until December 2024. Prior to that, he built his career both in Brazil and abroad at Petrobras, where he acted for 35 years, in addition to four years in the Naval Construction segment with the Brazilian Navy. Throughout his career at Petrobras, Mr. Travassos held several leadership positions, including Executive Director of Engineering, Technology and Innovation, Executive Manager of Deepwater Operations, and Executive Manager of Surface Systems, Refining, Gas, and Energy.

Jorge Boeri

Chief Onshore Operational Officer

Jorge Alejandro Boeri has a degree in Mechanical Engineering from the National University of Patagonia, a post-graduate degree in Project Management from the University of Belgrano, and participated in the Executive Development Program at the IAE Business School. Throughout his professional career, he has held various leadership positions, including Executive Manager at Brava Energia, Executive Manager at YPF (Argentina) and Operations Manager and Well Manager at Pan American Energy (Argentina). In addition, Boeri has been President of the IAPG (Instituto Argentino de Petroleo y Gas Seccional Sur) and of the LADS (Latin American Drilling Safety) South section in Argentina.
