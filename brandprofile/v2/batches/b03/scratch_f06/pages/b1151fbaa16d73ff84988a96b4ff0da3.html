Adris Grupa d.d. is a Croatian diversified conglomerate based in Rovinj.[3] It has more than 8,000 employees,[4] and is one of the leading companies in the region,[5][6] with an annual income of more than 5 billion kunas (around 1 bln dollars).[7]

Since the sale of Tvornica duhana Rovinj (TDR) to British American Tobacco in September 2015,[8] its core businesses are Maistra (tourism and hotels), Cromaris (food industry), and Croatia osiguranje (insurance).[9] It also operates a public internship and employment program called "The Future in Adris"[10] and The Adris Foundation, which awards scholarships and donations.[11]

It's being traded on the Zagreb Stock Exchange (ZSE) with a ticker symbol "ADRS".[12] In June 2022, Adris announced a one-year share buyback programme.[13]
