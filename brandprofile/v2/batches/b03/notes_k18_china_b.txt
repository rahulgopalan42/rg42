METHOD NOTES
- For ACFIC companies the revenue figure is the ACFIC list. Ownership was taken from Wikipedia or the company's own site. The legal form shown on the list
  (有限公司 / 有限责任公司) means the company itself cannot have exchange-traded shares, and this is cited where a page gave no ownership detail.
- Groups whose consumer brand trades through a listed subsidiary (Yutong, Semir, Weixing, Yongding, CSPC, Sunny, Gold Mantis) are left out or put in WATCH, as the brief requires.
- Moonshot AI has open compliance questions (Anthropic distillation allegations, a CAC investigation from Sep 2026). These are recorded and are not established findings.
- Xiaohongshu was preparing a HK IPO (June 2026). Before delivery, confirm that it has not listed since.
