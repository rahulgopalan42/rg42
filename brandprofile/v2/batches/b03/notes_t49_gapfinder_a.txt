NOTES t49_gapfinder_a (batch 3, 28 Nov 2026) - independent sector audit against GICS

== 1. GICS SUB-INDUSTRIES THAT NO BATCH 3 TASK COVERS ==
Source of the GICS list: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_Industry_Classification_Standard (163 sub-industries, read in full 28 Nov 2026).
Compared with the sector list at the top of v2/batches/b03/tasks.md and every task line in that file (sector t01-t53, subsidiaries u01-u08,
country k01-k30, clay, audit x01-x08). Country, subsidiary, clay and audit tasks cut across industries; they are not counted as covering an
industry unless their line names it. "Not covered" = no sector task line names the activity.

NOT COVERED by any task line (no task names it):
 1. Managed Health Care (health insurers / health plans, HMOs, statutory and private sickness funds). t09 names insurers, reinsurers, brokers,
    insurtech, assistance; it does not name health plans. Largest gap by revenue (US Blue Cross Blue Shield plans, Swiss and German sickness funds).
 2. Financial Exchanges & Data (stock and derivatives exchanges, clearing houses, central securities depositories, rating agencies,
    market-data and index providers). t04 covers asset managers / trading firms; t28 covers brokers and crypto exchanges; none names exchanges,
    clearing or financial data.
 3. Life Sciences Tools & Services (contract research organisations, lab instruments and consumables, clinical-trial technology).
    t26 names contract manufacturers only; t45 names testing laboratories (industrial TIC), not CROs.
 4. Commercial & Residential Mortgage Finance (non-bank mortgage lenders and servicers). t28 says "lenders" in a fintech sense only.
 5. Mortgage REITs (largely listed; low relevance).
 6. Household Products (detergents, cleaning, tissue, air care, insecticides). No b03 task names them (t23 covers beauty/personal care only).
 7. Housewares & Specialties (cookware, tableware, kitchen and storage products, candles). t21 names appliances, kitchens, tools; not housewares.
 8. Investment Banking & Brokerage (independent advisory banks, retail brokerage partnerships). t04 names private banks / wealth managers /
    trading firms; t28 online brokers; independent M&A advisory houses are named nowhere.
 9. Forest Products (timber, sawmills, lumber, wood panels, forestry owners) and Timber REITs. t11 covers pulp, paper and packaging only.
    (t50 lists "forestry" as an example, so overlap is possible.)
10. Coal & Consumable Fuels (coal miners, uranium). t06 says "mining" generically; t18 oil & gas; coal and uranium are named nowhere.
11. Construction Machinery & Heavy Transportation Equipment - the heavy transportation part: rolling stock, locomotives, railcars,
    buses' chassis makers are partly in t27 (trucks, buses); rail rolling-stock and railcar makers/lessors are named nowhere
    (t48 covers rail operators, not manufacturers).
12. Office Services & Supplies (office products, office furniture dealers, managed print, mailing equipment). t21 says "stationery" only.
13. Advertising (agencies, media-buying, out-of-home media owners, ad tech). No batch 3 SECTOR task names it (batch 2 had s05_marketing;
    clay_i14 pulls "Advertising Services" from Clay only).
14. Tobacco. No batch 3 sector task (batch 2 s03; clay_i22 pulls Tobacco Manufacturing). Low F1 usability (advertising law).
15. Oil & Gas Storage & Transportation (pipelines, midstream, storage terminals): t18 names LNG and trading, not pipelines/storage.
16. Self-Storage REITs/operators, Health Care REITs, Hotel & Resort REITs as owners: t13 names developers, investors, shopping centres,
    housebuilders, co-working, brokers; not self-storage, healthcare property or hotel-owning REITs.
17. Specialized Consumer Services (funeral, weddings, storage, personal services) - named only in t50's examples (t50 runs it).

PARTLY covered (named only in passing; worth a second look):
 - Health Care Supplies (gloves, consumables) - t26 "medical-device makers" only.
 - Communications Equipment (network gear, radios, antennas) - t15 says "hardware" generally; t14 connectors.
 - Other Specialty Retail / Homefurnishing Retail (sporting-goods, toy, book, pet, furniture retail chains) - t24 lists supermarkets, DIY,
   electronics, pharmacy, department stores, duty free; not sporting goods, furniture or pet retail.
 - Air Freight (cargo airlines) - t17 names forwarders and parcel; t31 airlines (passenger).
 - Biotechnology (non-venture, profitable private biotech) - t26 private pharma.
 - Diversified Support Services (uniform rental, auctioneers) - t45 partly.

Covered (for the record): all other GICS sub-industries map to at least one task line (t01-t53).
