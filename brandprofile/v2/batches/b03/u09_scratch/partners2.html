Built Differently to Build Differently

What We Do

We're business builders. Since our founding in 1996, Partners Group has invested more than USD 261 billion in private markets opportunities on behalf of our clients globally. We do this by drawing on a rich heritage of thinking differently. We have an industrial mindset that is more Main Street than Wall Street - and the kind of collaborative spirit you could only expect from a company with ‘partner’ in its name.
