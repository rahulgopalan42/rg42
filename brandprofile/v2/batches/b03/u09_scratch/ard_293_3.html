    Elée

    France

    21 January 2021

    Internet Software & Services
    Internet Software & Services

    Elée is one of the pioneers and the leading independent tech-enabled managed services provider of software asset management and cloud computing solutions in France. The company has developed an innovative tech-enabled offering that allows its customers to rapidly reduce costs, minimize their risk of non-compliance with software publishers and consistently optimize the management of their software and cloud assets.
    Elée has over 60 employees, and has developed its own proprietary software, Sambox.io, which has been acknowledged as one of the best solutions on the market at the ITAM Review Excellence Awards 2020. The company is the only French player that is part of market research company Gartner’s Magic Quadrant, which acknowledges the best companies in the sector worldwide. With 40% annual average revenue growth, Elée serves more than 100 clients, including the French State and over half of the CAC40 companies.

    France

    23 April 2020

    E-commerce
    E-commerce

    Cipanguo is an equipment manufacturer group with multiple entities including commercial companies Euro4x4, Japocat, specializing in the import and distribution of spare parts, equipment, and accessories for off-road vehicles. Euro4x4parts sells to both private and professional clients, with sales conducted via online channels and physical locations, which are strategically located in France and abroad.     Alsatis

    France

    04 February 2020

    Technology, Media and Telecom
    Technology, Media and Telecom

    Based in Toulouse, the Alsatis Group operates in the telecommunications sector in France and the United States through its subsidiaries Alsatis and Bloosurf.
    It specializes in the design, deployment and operation of state-of-the-art IP networks, fiber optics and radio, and related services (very high-speed broadband, VoIP, cybersecurity, cloud) for local communities, businesses and private individuals.
    The Alsatis Group was founded by aerospace engineers who instilled a strong technological culture. Its low-cost|fast-cycle approach and diversified teams guarantee flexibility and innovation in a fast-changing global sector. Alsatis Group was classified as an innovative company by BPI. Alsatis has integrated a French operational 4G-LTE solution, mainly in the Vendée region, and is now working on the transition to 5G, focusing on digital development in regions, IOT, Smart City and private LTE.

    France

    01 December 2019

    Business services
    Business services

    Founded in 1990, Sqorus (previously S&H) is a Digital Services Company specialized in consulting in the digital transformation of HR and Finance functions. The various consultants work with major accounts and ETIs on strategic projects, with an international dimension, around information systems: evolution strategy, help with choice, integration, Business Intelligence, Data Management, support and change management. The diversity of profiles, combining business knowledge and technical expertise, has been bringing real value to its customers for more than 30 years.
    But Sqorus is above all Talents dedicated to the success of projects and sharing strong values: diversity, commitment and solidarity. Great Place to Work for more than 5 consecutive years and winner of the Talent Award at the ESN 2018 Trophy, Sqorus is sensitive to the development of its employees, their career development and their training on solutions for the future. Sqorus has received the Oracle HCM Cloud 2019 Partner Award and the Oracle PaaS 2018 Award for its innovative projects and has won the trust of partner editors (Oracle, Talentsoft, NetSuite, Anaplan, Axway, Kyriba...).
