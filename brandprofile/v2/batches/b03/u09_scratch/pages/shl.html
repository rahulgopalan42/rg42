About us

We are the pioneering leader in self-injection solutions, dedicated to enabling patients’ independence in their healthcare journey.

Where independence begins

SHL Medical is the pioneering leader in designing, developing, and manufacturing self-injection solutions such as autoinjectors, pen injectors, and innovative specialty delivery systems for large-volume and high-viscosity formulations.

 

By partnering with leading pharma and biotech companies, we will continue to develop and supply products and services in order to support, engage, and oversee patients whose quality of life relies on innovative self-treatment therapies. This we do with one purpose in mind: Enabling patients' independence.

Roger Samuelsson and Martin Jelf establishes Scandinavian Health Limited (SHL Group).

world-leading autoinjector solutions provider

Commercial launch of SHL’s first DAI® project

The DAI becomes one of the most popular autoinjector formats on the market and lays the foundation for SHL’s success, supporting the modernization of self-injection treatment

Launch of Molly® autoinjector

The pre-configured Molly supports SHL's pharma partners in minimizing initial investments while maximizing development speed, later evolving into a modular platform with increased flexibility in design, development, and production.

Launch of final assembly, labeling, and packaging services in the US

New facility highlights SHL’s vertically integrated approach to drug delivery services, offering streamlined operations from product design to final delivery.

1.5 billion autoinjectors shipped

SHL Medical has reached a historic milestone, enabling patients’ independence 1.5 billion times through our self-injection solutions worldwide. This achievement reflects decades of teamwork, innovation, and trust, as well as the confidence our pharmaceutical and biotech partners place in us. With 1.5 billion autoinjectors shipped globally, we celebrate not only the scale of our impact, but also the shared commitment that continues to drive innovation in drug delivery and improve the lives of millions of patients around the world.

Built for sustainable growth

We have embarked on a journey to embed sustainability into how we operate, innovate, and grow, turning ambition into measurable action that supports long‑term value. Guided by our 2030 Sustainability Strategy, we focus on real progress: expanding patient access while reducing emissions, integrating circular design into our products, increasing renewable energy use, and strengthening ethical practices across our supply chain.

This is not about commitments on paper, but about implementation across our business, enabling patient independence, building operational resilience, and creating tangible, lasting impact for patients, partners, and society.
