Our Mission: To Positively Impact the Financial Lives of Our Clients

Request an Introduction

Our name, Cerity Partners, reflects our steadfast commitment to enhancing the financial well-being of individuals and their families, businesses and their employees and nonprofit organizations through objective advice and oversight. As part of this commitment, we have brought together a team of specialists– financial planners, investment professionals, tax advisors, and retirement plan consultants – dedicated to helping clients achieve their short- and long-term goals. We continually seek strategic opportunities to expand the breadth and depth of our firm to meet the evolving needs of our clients and to provide diverse perspectives and insights.

At Cerity Partners We Are:

  • Collaborative.
  • Entrepreneurial.
  • Resolute.
  • In-Service Mindset.
  • Thought-Leaders.
  • You.

We believe that collaborating with colleagues surrounds our clients with the expertise to provide superior advice-driven service and solutions. We demonstrate collaboration by including multiple team members in all areas of what we do, whether client facing or focused on the business. We hold ourselves and our colleagues accountable for what we promise and what we deliver. We think about succession and ensure that there is extensive talent and expertise available to our clients.

We are not employees, nor do we act like employees. We are colleagues and business owners who appreciate that the returns on our investment in the business benefit all. We are innovative and proactive and consciously make decisions about our practice areas with more than ourselves in mind.

We are unwavering in our commitment to put clients first and to minimize conflicts of interest whenever possible. We act with integrity, authenticity and purpose to be a steward and advisor for our clients and colleagues.

We are in-service to clients, colleagues, and the firm.

We demonstrate our commitment to our practice by developing expertise and knowledge that is unmatched. Our growth-mindset means we consistently explore the universe of advice and are open to feedback and information that can help us to be the best advisors, colleagues and partners. We lead by example.

We believe in the power of thinking of others first and strive to create something bigger than the sum of its individual parts.

Welcome to Cerity Partners. Our name is a modification of the word sincerity, which encapsulates our character and the experience our clients receive. We look forward to working with you.

Kurt Miscinski, Partner & Chief Executive Officer

We believe in people, encourage diverse perspectives, and foster a culture of excellence.
