Built to Transform How Revenue Gets Done.

Xactly was founded in 2005 on a simple conviction: that sales teams perform better when their incentives are clear, fair, and data-driven. Two decades later, that conviction has grown into the world's first Intelligent Revenue Platform, built on 21+ years of proprietary compensation and performance data that no competitor can replicate.

We've spent more than 20 years in the room with revenue leaders through market shifts, economic cycles, and the AI revolution. We know what it takes to build plans that motivate sellers, forecasts that hold up, and compensation structures that drive the right behavior. That's not a feature. That's the foundation.

We Lead With C.A.R.E.

Xactly prides itself on our core C.A.R.E. values (Customer Focus, Accountability, Respect, and Excellence.) And these principles guide us in all that we do.

Customer Focus

We work to be a trusted partner, not just a vendor. Every product decision and touchpoint starts with the customer to meet and exceed their expectations.

Our History. Your Advantage.

True innovation requires a strong foundation. Our timeline is more than a record of our past, but rather the engine driving our future. We didn’t just witness the evolution of revenue management: we pioneered it. Since 2005, our relentless drive to challenge the status quo has fueled continuous adaptation and strategic expansion. That two-decade journey gives us the resilient footing to anticipate market shifts and empower revenue leaders for whatever comes next. Today, we leverage our deep roots and unmatched experience as the ultimate launchpad to build the future of Intelligent Revenue.
