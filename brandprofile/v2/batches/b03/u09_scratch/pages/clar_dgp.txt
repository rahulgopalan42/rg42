Clarience Technologies Announces Partnership with the 2025 Chevrolet Detroit Grand Prix for a Third Year
Southfield, MI
(May 30th, 2025) – Clarience Technologies has announced its partnership with the Chevrolet Detroit Grand Prix presented by Lear for a third year. The 35
th
running of the Chevrolet Detroit Grand Prix returns May 30-June 1, 2025, through an exhilarating 1.7 mile, nine-turn circuit on the streets of downtown Detroit.
Clarience Technologies will have an incredible presence at the Detroit Grand Prix this year, as a sponsor of both Team Penske and AJ Foyt Racing for some of NTT INDYCAR racing’s most talented drivers. Clarience Technologies is a primary sponsor of AJ Foyt Racing’s Car No. 4 driven by David Malukas, coming off a thrilling second place finish in last weekend’s 2025 Indy 500.  The company is also supporting Team Penske with their name displayed on the No. 2 Chevrolet car driven by Josef Newgarden, the 2024 Indy 500 Champion.
“Clarience Technologies is proud to sponsor AJ Foyt Racing and Team Penske at the 2025 Detroit Grand Prix and last weekend at the 109th running of the Indianapolis 500,” said Brian Kupchella, Clarience Technologies, Chief Executive Officer.  “Our company is headquartered in Michigan so anytime we get a chance to participate in a local Detroit event, it’s extra special.  We are excited to be onsite this weekend cheering on the team.”
For Clarience Technologies it’s not just about the cars on the track – they proudly support safety and visibility efforts for drivers and teams alike. Ranging from the INDYCAR organization & Team Penske transport vehicles hauling cars from race to race around the country you may find products from the Clarience Technologies’ Team of Companies at work. Truck-Lite products are featured on the INDYCAR organization medical trailers, media trailers, and trailers hauling production equipment, while the AMR safety vehicles are fully equipped with Code 3 lighting and their Matrix System. In addition to the IndyCar Hauler fleet, they proudly support the Team Penske Race trailers with Truck-Lite and Rigid Lighting products.
About Clarience Technologies:
Clarience Technologies focuses on developing vehicle and fleet management technology innovations for the commercial vehicle, passenger car, recreational off-road machine, and pleasure craft markets. Through our data and insights, we improve fleet management, streamline supply chain management, and enhance safety on and off the road. Since our Truck-Lite brand introduced the first sealed marker light in 1955, our companies have unveiled a steady stream of innovations that have turned customers into long-term partners. Today, Clarience Technologies is a global company known for its quality and dedication to sustainability – and to providing the technologies that keep our world moving forward. Learn more at
www.clariencetechnologies.com
