Manufacturing Excellence with European Transparency and Supply Chain Security

European Leader in Data Storage & Security

When industrial systems operate in extreme conditions, when supply chains demand documented regulatory compliance, or when defense contracts require European manufacturing, you need more than standard components. You need a partner with decades of experience in tailored applications where failure isn't an option.

Swissbit is the only European manufacturer of industrial-grade storage and security solutions with advanced in-house electronics production in Berlin. Founded in 2001, we serve customers in regulated, demanding markets where reliability, security, and supply chain transparency define success.

What Sets Swissbit Apart

Complete In-House Development

We design and develop both hardware and firmware internally. This end-to-end control allows us to optimize storage performance, customize solutions for specific applications, and maintain full transparency over our products. Unlike many competitors who rely on third-party controllers and standard firmware, Swissbit creates tailored solutions that address the exact requirements of industrial environments.

Advanced Packaging Technology

Our System-in-Package (SiP) expertise enables us to transform bare semiconductor chips into robust, finished components. Through chip-on-board (COB) and surface-mount technology (SMT) in our Berlin facility, we produce customized storage solutions with enhanced reliability and extended temperature ranges.

Integrated Security by Design

Security isn’t an afterthought—it’s engineered into our products from the ground up. We combine hardware-based encryption, secure boot, and protected key storage to safeguard data at rest and device integrity. This approach is backed by ISO/IEC 27001 and aligned with European and international security standards, supporting requirements such as NIS2.

European Manufacturing & Supply Chain

Many Swissbit storage and security products are manufactured in our Berlin, Germany Fab under strict quality and environmental standards. For customers concerned about supply chain security, geopolitical risks, and compliance with European regulations, our European manufacturing provides an alternative to far-east-dependent supply chains. Our Berlin facility, with more than 10,000 m² of production space, is part of the IPCEI ME/CT initiative, Europe’s largest semiconductor program.

Our Vision & Mission

Vision: Building a connected world where data and identities are trusted to ensure digital sovereignty.

Mission: We provide industry-leading data storage and security solutions that ensure reliable storage, data protection, and secure access for critical applications. This enables our customers to accelerate digital transformation across various industries.

Digital sovereignty, the ability to control and secure data within trusted, transparent systems, matters more than ever in an era of rising cyber threats, supply chain vulnerabilities, and geopolitical tensions. Swissbit contributes to this goal through European manufacturing, transparent development processes, and technologies that put control back in the hands of our customers.

Growth Through Strategic Partnerships

Over the years, Swissbit has built and continuously strengthened strategic partnerships across its supply chain, especially with key flash, controller, and component suppliers. These long-term collaborations reinforce stable access to critical technologies, support consistent product quality, and help accelerate innovation across our portfolio. 

The 2021 acquisition of Hyperstone, a German developer of NAND flash controllers, extended our expertise throughout the storage value chain. Hyperstone's controller technology complements Swissbit's firmware and manufacturing capabilities, enabling deeper optimization and differentiation in our product portfolio. Now, Swissbit employs over 500 specialists across R&D, production, and customer support.

Our Berlin manufacturing facility received recognition in 2023 as part of the IPCEI ME/CT program (Important Projects of Common European Interest in Microelectronics and Communication Technologies). This European Union initiative supports strategic semiconductor projects to strengthen Europe's technological sovereignty and reduce dependency on non-European suppliers.
