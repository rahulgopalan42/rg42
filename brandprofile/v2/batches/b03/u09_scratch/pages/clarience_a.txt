Meet Clarience Technologies
Built for the Future of Transportation
Clarience Technologies is a global leader in transportation safety, visibility and digital technologies – bringing together trusted companies, proven expertise and innovative solutions that help keep people, assets and businesses safe, secure and productive.
Our Mission
is to provide
Total Visibility
to transportation through technologies that keep the world moving forward. We pursue this mission because we understand lives and livelihoods depend on it.
Keeping people safe. Assets and cargo secure. Businesses more productive. That’s what
Total Visibility
means in practice.
A Portfolio of Proven Company Brands.
Clarience Technologies was created in 2020 to unite trusted transportation company brands around a shared purpose: advancing safety and visibility. From the companies that pioneered LED lighting for commercial vehicles to the technologies redefining fleet intelligence, we bring it all together under one roof. By combining deep market knowledge with strengths in engineering, manufacturing and digital technology, our team of companies can innovate faster in a rapidly changing world and deliver more complete, connected solutions. Together, we are helping transportation meet today’s demands and preparing for what comes next.
Explore Our Brands
Safety, Visibility & Digital Solutions for Transportation
Across our diverse portfolio, we deliver solutions in three core areas: Safety, Visibility and Digital Technologies. Our solutions include lighting, camera and vision systems, tire management, fuel filtration, fire and emergency equipment, storage and upfitting, cargo protection and fleet telematics. Individually, each solution addresses a critical challenge. Together, they form an ecosystem built to improve safety, increase visibility and optimize performance across the most demanding environments in transportation.
Our Solutions
The Scale to Serve the World. The Focus to Serve You.
Our global footprint spans North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific and beyond – giving customers everywhere access to local expertise backed by world-class innovation and a dependable supply chain.
We Serve 18+ Transportation Industries
Clarience Technologies supports more than 18 transportation industries that keep the world moving from trucking, work vehicles and transit to law enforcement, fire and emergency and beyond. Across these industries, our safety, visibility and digital technologies solve complex challenges such as helping drivers and operators stay safe in demanding environments and helping fleets stay productive – reducing downtime through connectivity, predictive maintenance and real-time visibility that keeps assets on the road and businesses moving.
