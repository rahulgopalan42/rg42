Turning Complexity into Connectivity
For more than 25 years, OEC has been the digital backbone of the vehicle repair and supply ecosystem, powering connections across the largest network of repairers, parts suppliers, and vehicle owners globally.

OUR ORIGIN
Built by the industry, for the industry.
Back in 2000, leading auto manufacturers partnered to create OEC, keeping vehicles, dealers, and repairers connected to genuine parts.
25 years later, our founding principles still shape the industry we serve.
OEM Origin
2000
Ford, GM, Chrysler, and Bell & Howell (now Snap‑on Business Solutions) launched OEC to solve a critical industry problem.
Marketplace
2015
OEC became the largest online parts marketplace in North America and began global expansion.
Workflow
2020
Our solutions expanded across the complete vehicle parts and repair workflow.
Digital Backbone
Today & Tomorrow
We are the connective digital tissue behind the global repair economy.
