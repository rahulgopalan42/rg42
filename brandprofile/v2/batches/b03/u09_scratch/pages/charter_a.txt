Who Is CNG?
We are Enhancing Lives
for a Better World
We Are Not Just Any Company. We Are CNG.
At Charter Next Generation, tomorrow is our mission because we are not satisfied with today. We are leading the charge, pushing the limits, and tackling the world’s toughest challenges.
Explore what we do
Our Purpose
Enhancing Lives
Reducing waste, eliminating greenhouse gas emissions, shrinking the wealth gap – CNG defines success in these terms, by the number of lives we positively affect. It’s our highest ambition – a goal that sets our course as a company and gives meaning to our work across four pillars:
People
Through ownership, we empower our people to fulfill our commitments and contribute to reducing the wealth gap.
Planet
From the design of our products to the management of our facilities, we lead with sustainable solutions that lower greenhouse gas emissions and reduce waste.
Communities
Our sustainable products extend shelf life and help deliver fresh food all over the world. We continue to invest in communities where we live and work through our Community Giving Fund.
Customers
We pursue our mission of providing best-in-class material science solutions, and deliver on our promise to accelerate our customers’ business growth.
Our Vision
Providing Solutions for a Better World
Our pillars are not just an explanation of our purpose but a lens through which we view the world and explore how specialty films, material science solutions, and community involvement enhance lives. They are a roadmap that we follow every day as we chart our course toward a better tomorrow.
Our Mission
Delivering Best-in-Class Material Science Solutions
Expertise in material science allows CNG to address critical challenges across industries. Our solutions preserve food, reduce waste, improve energy efficiency, address the wage gap, and protect the environment through advanced materials and continuous innovation. As material science experts, we are committed to advancing sustainability, circularity, and a low-carbon future.

Our Values
The Guiding Principles that Define CNG
Our purpose goes beyond profit-making. It defines the fundamental reason for our existence and articulates our broader impact on society. Employees who feel a strong sense of purpose are more likely to be engaged and committed, leading to higher retention rates and a stronger company. By fostering an ownership mindset, we enhance our ability to serve our customers, drive business growth, and create meaningful impact. We build this mindset company-wide through six guiding values that act as a north star for every employee.
Committed
We act with integrity and respect for one another.
Collaborative
We are better when we work together.
Caring
We strive to be socially conscious in all we do.
Courageous
We take risks to achieve better outcomes.
Curious
We encourage new thinking to reimagine what’s possible.
Competitive
We play to win.
Legends in the Making
Our people are the foundation of Charter Next Generation. Through our partnership with Ownership Works®, every employee of CNG is a part-owner. We know the value in taking ownership of the future, and we harness the power of our employee-owners to tackle the world’s toughest challenges, always with an eye on sustainability and emerging packaging trends. They are the people making food fresher, homes safer, and the environment cleaner.
CNG leads the industry because we never stop innovating. And with every new innovation, our people’s legend only grows.
Meet the Owners
Passionately Pursuing a Sustainable Future
As CNG continues to grow, we are leading the charge in delivering innovative and sustainable material science. Because there is no future for CNG if there is no future for planet earth. We are reducing our customers’ carbon footprints with innovative film solutions, supporting our communities through employee-ownership and equity initiatives, and setting new standards for sustainability in our industry.
Innovation across the Nation
18 state-of-the-art facilities, at 11 unique locations, with 1 singular goal: to make the world a better place.
