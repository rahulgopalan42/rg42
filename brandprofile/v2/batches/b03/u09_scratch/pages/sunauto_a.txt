About Us
Trusted Automotive Experts
for Nearly 100 Years
Sun Auto Tire & Service has delivered expert tire and automotive care through a growing network of 575+ locations nationwide for nearly 100 years. What began as a single service center in 1928 has grown into a trusted network of over 575 locations nationwide.
Our certified technicians bring top-tier expertise to every visit, whether you need new tires, oil changes, brake repairs, or advanced engine service. With courtesy Digital Vehicle Inspections, a nationwide warranty, and payment options that fit your budget, we make car care clear, easy, and built around you.
At Sun Auto, We Keep You in the Driver’s Seat with local service you can trust and coverage that goes wherever the road takes you.
Built on Trust. Backed by Experience.
With over 500 locations nationwide, we’re proud to be the choice for drivers who expect more from their car care.
Our certified technicians follow rigorous standards for every service, backed by hands-on training and a commitment to Clarity, Confidence, and Customer care. Whether it’s your first visit or your fiftieth, you can count on consistent, quality service from a team to Keep You in the Driver’s Seat.
High-Quality Tire & Auto Service with Transparent Pricing
At Sun Auto Network, you get expert repairs and scheduled maintenance for all makes and models, delivered with transparency, value, and care. We recommend only the services your vehicle truly needs, nothing more and nothing less. With written guarantees, clear pricing, and a Courtesy Digital Vehicle Inspection included with every visit, we make it easy to stay ahead of maintenance and drive with confidence.
