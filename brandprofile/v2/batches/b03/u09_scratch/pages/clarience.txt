We are Clarience Technologies.
LEADING TRANSPORTATION FORWARD
When facing the unknown, don’t just look ahead.  Be visionary.
Our leading visibility and safety technologies include cameras and vision systems, emergency warning systems, cargo protection equipment and much more. Yet, the products we started with continue to inspire us most. For decades, our lights have provided the visibility to make transportation safer. They’ve also given us the vision so we never stop looking past the horizon to seek new ways for technology to help keep people, assets and businesses safe, secure and productive.
Get to Know Us
ADVANCING SAFETY
We help protect what matters to you, no matter what you haul.
How do our customers define safety?  A school transportation director will tell you it is about protecting students on their entire journey to school. A food distributor will tell you safety is the assurance that shipments remain at a constant temperature in transit. We understand that safety is different for each of our customers. With solutions for every segment of the transportation market, we are committed to advancing safety for every customer, and every journey.

PURSUING TOTAL VISIBILITY
What if you had the power to see everything?
We believe the key to advancing transportation is vision – literally. That’s why we pursue Total Visibility™ – to help our customers see everything so they are prepared for anything. We integrate the latest electronic hardware with modern software platforms to create a connected ecosystem of technologies at work, providing our customers a more complete view of any kind of fleet. This IoT-based approach is behind the future vision of each the Clarience Technologies brands.

Creating safer electric vehicles with sound.
The ECCO Electrical Vehicle Alert System (EVAS®) emits an audible warning to alert pedestrians to electric, hybrid, and commercial vehicles driving at low speeds. Custom tones include acceleration, deceleration and user experience tones such as charge complete, door open, power on or power off.
Explore ECCO
Smarter LED technology adapts to a changing environment.
The first layer of adaptive response is RIGID’s Active Power Management™ (APM) system which continuously monitors input voltage to determine the status of the vehicle’s electrical system. RIGID’s proven Active View Technology uses an internal GPS module to determine the vehicle’s speed, allowing the Adapt E-Series to properly distribute all available power among three unique optic zones. An optimized, off-road experience, in all conditions.
