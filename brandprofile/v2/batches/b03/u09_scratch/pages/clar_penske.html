Team Penske Grows Relationship with Clarience Technologies

Transportation Technology Leader Will be Featured on No. 3 and No. 12 Chevrolets in the NTT INDYCAR SERIES

Mooresville, NC (February 17, 2026) – Team Penske and Clarience Technologies announced today that the global leader of visibility, safety and digital technologies for transportation will increase its visibility with the team’s NTT INDYCAR SERIES (NICS) operation for the 2026 season and beyond, building upon a relationship that began in 2023 at the Chevrolet Detroit Grand Prix.

The Clarience Technologies logo will be featured in two primary liveries on the No. 3 Chevrolet driven by Scott McLaughlin at Detroit on May 31 and the first race of the Milwaukee Mile doubleheader on August 29. The logo will be featured on the fin for the full season, while also adorning the fin of No. 12 Chevrolet driven by David Malukas for the full season.

“Clarience Technologies is an industry-leading brand that is a welcomed addition to our INDYCAR SERIES primary partner lineup,” said Penske Racing, Inc. President, Jonathan Diuguid. “It is always great to see a relationship grow organically because it means the partnership is mutually beneficial. We look forward to getting them to Victory Lane this season.”

McLaughlin is a seven-time NICS winner since joining the series full-time in 2021, including a 2024 win at Milwaukee. The New Zealand-American has finished inside the top 10 in the NICS standings in each of the last four seasons and holds the four-lap qualifying record for the Indianapolis 500, which he set in 2024 when Team Penske swept the top three positions.

“Clarience Technologies is another great new partner for the No. 3 Team Penske Chevrolet,” said McLaughlin. “They are a proud brand on the rise, just like the INDYCAR SERIES. We take a lot of pride in getting new partners their first win and I’m confident we can do that for Clarience Technologies.”

Malukas will embark on his first NICS season as a member of Team Penske in 2026. The dynamic young driver finished second in the Indianapolis 500 in 2025 supported by Clarience Technologies, and is one of the rising talents in the series.

“We are excited to partner again with Team Penske and look forward to an exciting season ahead,” said Brian Kupchella, Clarience Technologies, Chief Executive Officer. “Further, we take pride in supporting race day with safety and visibility products from our team of companies - from trailers transporting equipment to the races to visibility products on the safety vehicles on the track.”

The 2026 NTT INDYCAR SERIES season will begin Sunday, March 1 on the streets of St. Peterburg, Florida. Coverage of the full season can be seen on FOX.

About Clarience Technologies:

Clarience Technologies is a global leader of visibility, safety and digital technologies for transportation. Built on the foundation of proven company brands each with a legacy of innovation, its solutions include vehicle lighting, camera and vision systems, telematics and safety solutions that protect our world and our livelihoods by keeping people, assets and businesses safe, secure and productive. Its team of companies includes Al-Van, AMFS, American Van, Code 3, DAVCO, Durite, ECCO, Elkhart Brass, Foam Pro, FRC, Kerr, Labcraft, LED Autolamps, Lumitec, Pressure Systems International, Prime Design, Randall, Ranger Design, RIGID, Road-Ready, Roll-Rite, R.O.M., RVS, Safe Fleet (Law, Bus & Rail), SEON, Truck-Lite and Vango. For more information, visit www.clariencetechnologies.com.

About Team Penske:

Team Penske is one of the most successful teams in the history of professional sports and is celebrating its 60th Anniversary during the 2026 season. From its first race in the1966 24 Hours of Daytona, cars owned and prepared by Team Penske have produced more than 650 major race wins, over 700 pole positions and 48 National Championships across open-wheel, stock car and sports car racing competition. In its storied history, the team has also earned 20 Indianapolis 500 victories, three Daytona 500 Championships, a Formula 1 win, victories in the 24 Hours of Daytona and the 12 Hours of Sebring, along with a win in Australia's legendary Bathurst 1000 race. More than 100 drivers have raced for Team Penske in its six decades. The team currently competes in the NASCAR Cup Series and NTT INDYCAR Series, as well as IMSA WeatherTech SportsCar Championship through its global partnership, Porsche Penske Motorsport. For more information about Team Penske, please visit www.teampenske.com.
