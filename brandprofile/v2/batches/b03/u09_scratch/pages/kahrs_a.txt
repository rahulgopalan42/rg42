Our offering
Our products are engineered to deliver long-lasting performance, meeting a wide range of design and functional preferences for projects of any kind and scale – supported by a seamless, digitally enabled customer journey and a commitment to adding value at every step.
Floors for Residential use
The right flooring plays a central role in shaping the interior of a home. It should offer visual appeal, comfort underfoot and functionality for everyday use while also promoting a heathy indoor environment.
Kährs offer a broad assortment of floors to suit all tastes and interiors, ranging from warm, natural wood floors in different formats and patterns to vinyl floors with all the durability and advantages of modern flooring technology.

Floors for commercial use
Within Kährs’ wide range there are flooring solutions for most hardwearing and specific needs.
Looking for flooring for healthcare, schools, shops and restaurants. Our floors are durable, resistant to chemicals and stains, easy to maintain which gives favorable life cycle cost, and, we have a wide range of PVC-free options.

Kährs Group at a glance
Kährs Group is a manufacturer and distributor of premium flooring for residential and commercial spaces. With innovations that have shaped the industry over generations, Kährs offers flooring solutions for every room, environment and need. Kährs delivers products designed to last to customers in nearly 70 countries.
