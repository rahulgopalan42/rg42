Company Portrait
ARVOS Group is a leading global industrial equipment and service provider. The group is active in both the new equipment and aftermarket service markets across its two divisions
•
LJUNGSTRÖM for Air Preheaters and Gas-Gas Heaters primarily used in thermal power generation facilities and new products in Offshore wind and other diversification product areas
•
SCHMIDTSCHE SCHACK for heat transfer applications in a wide range of petrochemical, chemical and metallurgical processes
Headquartered in Luxembourg, with the German holding company and the corporate offices in Heidelberg, ARVOS Group operates six global manufacturing facilities and has sales execution offices in Australia, China, Czech Republic, Germany, India, Japan, Poland and the USA. The ARVOS Group brands of LJUNGSTRÖM®, SCHMIDT’SCHE ® and SCHACK® are highly recognized for representing outstanding quality and performance. ARVOS Group, owned by funds advised by Triton, has more than 1,000 employees worldwide.
