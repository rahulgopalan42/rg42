We are Cérélia
Cérélia is the market leader in ready-to-bake doughs (for tarts, pizzas and Viennese pastries), and also has recognized expertise in the manufacture of crêpes, pancakes, waffles and cookies.
With 16production sites, including 12 in Europe and 4 in North America, Cérélia markets its products in over 50 countries through its own brands, as well as private labels, industrial partners and Foodservice.

We are Cérélia
Leader sur le marché des pâtes prêtes à cuire (pâtes à tarte, à pizza, à viennoiserie), Cérélia dispose également d’un savoir-faire reconnu dans la fabrication de crêpes & pancakes, flatbread et cookies. Ses 12 sites de production, dont 9 en Europe (France, Belgique, Pays-Bas, Italie, Royaume-Uni) et 3 en Amérique du Nord (Canada et USA), approvisionnent les 50 pays dans lesquels ses produits sont distribués en marques en propres, MDD et co-manufacturing.
We care
Aware of the ecological challenges of our time, we act to improve our environmental impact and mobilize resources in the most efficient way. We find solutions and share them with the players in our industry, to maximize their impact.
At Cérélia, CSR is a story of men and women with shared beliefs, which translates into concrete, strong and engaging actions.
