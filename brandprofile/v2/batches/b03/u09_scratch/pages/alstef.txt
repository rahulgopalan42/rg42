Our automated material handling solutions
Alstef Group designs, integrates and supports automated material handling solutions for airports, air cargo, parcel logistics and warehouses worldwide. Our expertise includes airport baggage handling systems, air cargo handling systems, warehouse automation, automated guided vehicles (AGVs), robotics, sortation, conveying, storage and control software. With decades of experience in automation, Alstef delivers integrated solutions designed to improve operational efficiency, safety, throughput and reliability throughout the material handling process.
