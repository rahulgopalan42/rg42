Royal Reesink, a leading global family of machinery equipment distributors
Royal Reesink is a global leader in the distribution, service and lifecycle support of high-quality machinery. Since 1786, we have evolved from a blacksmith workshop into a trusted distribution partner connecting manufacturers, customers and markets worldwide.
We operate across key sectors including agriculture, turfcare, construction, material handling and logistics solutions, supporting industries that help feed, build, move and maintain the world around us.
Global strength, local focus
With 35 operating companies across four continents, 2,250 employees and annual revenue of €1.1 billion, we combine global scale with local expertise. Our distributor platform gives our subsidiaries and dealerships the strength of an international group while staying close to the customers and markets they serve.
Enabling your business
At Royal Reesink, we do more than distribute machinery. We enable businesses to perform at their best.
For customers, we guarantee operational continuity and maximize lifecycle value through trusted advice, operator training, original parts, maintenance and technical support. For OEM partners, we provide a strong route to market through our local presence, market knowledge and customer relationships. Our operating companies benefit from the scale and shared capabilities of a global organisation, while remaining close to the customers they serve.
