    No one knows color like nature

    Oterra is the world’s top natural food color provider, offering the widest portfolio since 1876. Our leadership comes from unmatched R&D, application expertise, and full control over raw material value chains—delivering nature’s true colors to food and beverage industries.

    Because nature got it right the first time

    Demand for safe, natural food is at an all-time high – and consumers want food and beverage manufacturers to offer ingredients that are naturally sourced and free from anything artificial.  

    At Oterra, we answer the demand for natural. We want to make it easy for everyone to enjoy good food that is naturally colored and appealing. Because everyone deserves food that’s safe to eat and made from natural ingredients they can trust.  

    Our reach is global

    Wherever you are, we can help you produce compliant products for local markets or for export around the globe.

    Our application and customer support teams constantly exchange knowledge. This means that no matter where you are or what your challenge is, somewhere in the world we have addressed a natural food color challenge like yours and have the expertise to help you solve it.
