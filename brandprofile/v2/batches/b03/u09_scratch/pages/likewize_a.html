About Us

The best device programs run on Likewize.

We operate in the high-stakes moments where devices fail, expectations are high, and brands are judged. From built-to-order protection and rapid repairs to global renewal and digital support, we provide the infrastructure required to turn technical setbacks into long-term revenue for the world’s leading telcos, retailers, and financial institutions.

Tech is everywhere. So are we.

We believe customer loyalty is won when brands meet technology needs in the moments that matter most. By owning these critical device moments with speed, transparency, and consistency, we provide the global infrastructure that the world's biggest brands depend on to keep their customers connected. We are Likewize—the power behind the best device programs in the world.
