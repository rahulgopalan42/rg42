Let's talk
Decades of Data, One Global Network,
Uniquely Independent.
If we were an airline, we would be
the world’s largest
. At JSSI, we focus exclusively on business aviation — advocating for our clients is always at the center of everything we do.
Our expert team is available to assist at anytime and anywhere in the world — adding a layer of technical expertise and connecting our vast global network of service providers to your every maintenance event.
We leverage decades of maintenance data and product line experience to power a unique product portfolio to support the aircraft life cycle from acquisition and operation to end-of-life services.
Our independence from OEMs means our only priority is you. We serve as a single point of contact across assets and mixed fleets, delivering flexible solutions aligned with your operational goals.

Driving
Innovation
Since 1989, JSSI has led innovation in business aviation — introducing industry-first programs like Pro-Rata and Tip-to-Tail® coverage, and building one of the largest and most diverse parts inventories and engine leasing pools. Through investments in maintenance tracking and data, we’re committed to pushing boundaries with our clients in focus.
Empowering
Flexibility
Business aviation is not a one-size-fits-all industry, and neither are our solutions. Every JSSI program and solution is tailored to your aircraft, mission profile, and budget. Whether you operate a single aircraft or manage a diverse fleet, our flexible products, options, and bundled services are designed to meet your unique needs.
