The leading parts distributor worldwide.

Founded in 1985, Arrowhead Electrical Parts has become a worldwide distributor of non-discretionary, mission-critical aftermarket replacement parts. We are now trusted by over 50,000 dealers, distributors, repairs shops and small businesses to provide quality, fit and selection.

Our global footprint includes 10 distribution centers in North America (nine in the US and one in Canada), as well as one in the UK; plus sourcing offices in India and China. These state-of-the-art facilities not only put us at the forefront of production, but allow us to continually expand our product portfolio and reach new geographical markets.
