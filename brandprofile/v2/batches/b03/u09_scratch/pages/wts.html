About Us

With representation in over 100 countries, WTS Global has already grown to a leadership position as a global tax practice offering the full range of tax services and aspires to become the preeminent non-audit tax practice worldwide. WTS Global deliberately refrains from conducting annual audits in order to avoid any conflicts of interest and to be the long- term trusted advisor for its international clients. Clients of WTS Global include multinational companies, international mid-size companies as well as private clients and family offices.

We are Locally rooted - Globally connected

The member firms of WTS Global are carefully selected through stringent quality reviews. They are strong local players in their home market who are united by the ambition of building a truly global practice that develops the tax leaders of the future and anticipates the new digital tax world.

WTS Global effectively combines senior tax expertise from different cultures and backgrounds and offers world-class skills in advisory, in-house, regulatory and digital, coupled with the ability to think like experienced business people in a constantly changing world.

What makes us unique

Tax focussed

We offer a full fledged tax service portfolio specializing in all domains of taxation and advising corporate, private and institutional clients. The tax services range from Corporate Tax to Indirect Tax, Customs, Transfer Pricing & Valuations Services,  M&A (Tax), Global Expatriate Services, International Tax and Permanent Establishments (PEs), Tax Controversy, Financial Services, Real Estate (Tax) and Digital Solutions. All member firms are bound to render the full set of global services as a minimum catalogue on a national level. 

Corporate organization

We are much more than a network. We have similar structures to a corporate organization, including a dedicated full-time central management team which is led by a CEO who, until recently, was Head of Tax at a multinational company. Our global Service Line Heads and Regional Coordinators ensure that our member firms have regular technical exchanges on a global, regional and national level.

No audits

Since our foundation in 2003, we have continuously focussed on tax consulting and deliberately refrained from annual audits in order to avoid potential conflicts of interest. With this strategic decision, we anticipated today’s demands of the European Union to separate tax consulting from audit services at an early stage. As all member firms of WTS Global share the principal of refraining from conducting annual audits, we lay the foundation for a trustful, long-termrelationship with our clients which is geared towards the future.

Exclusivity

We have a ‘one firm per country’ policy. It is our aim to have trustful and long-lasting relationships with our member firms. However, in exceptional and rare cases, due to market size or any other substantial reason where it is crucial to have more than one partner per country, we allow exceptions to the rule.

Hand-picked partner firms

We select our member firms very carefully. Not only do we conduct stringent quality reviews prior to admission into the network and in the course of membership to guarantee highest quality standards, but we also devote significant time and effort to get to know the people behind the company. A large number of our partners come from big global tax consultancies who decided to establish their own firms, thus, adding an entrepreneurial flavour to our network.

Our values and what we stand for

We are connected by our shared values, which are the foundation on which we base our daily actions upon. These values form the basis for our interaction with clients, member firms and colleagues. From professional to senior partner, we live our values both externally and internally. They determine our collaboration within WTS Global. We are proud of that.

1/4

Our culture is characterised by openness, an entrepreneurial spirit and curiosity about new developments and trends. With creativity and dynamism, we are constantly developing new ideas to meet ever-changing challenges. We are involved in pioneering work and anticipate new developments in the market at an early stage. We are committed to digitization and take an active role in the development of IT tax tools.

Passionate

2/4
