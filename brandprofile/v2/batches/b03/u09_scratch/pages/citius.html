      Our Journey

      The path that shaped us

      2005 - 2007

      Founding vision and the first engineering core

      CitiusTech began with a clear mission: build a values-driven company focused entirely on healthcare technology. The founders assembled a high-caliber engineering team and set the principles that continue to anchor our culture and growth. 

      2008-2010

      Specialized capability building 

      Our community expanded with more than one hundred engineers, strengthening our depth in healthcare technology and establishing CitiusTech as a niche healthcare partner.

      2011 - 2013

      Early scale and industry recognition 

      We crossed two hundred professionals and gained recognition as healthcare technology innovators, backed by strong partnerships and highquality solutions.

      2014 - 2016

      Acceleration and global validation

      This period marked rapid growth backed by awardwinning innovation. We earned multiple Stevie Awards and secured investment from General Atlantic, while deepening our product engineering and data capabilities. 

      2017 - 2023

      Global expansion and strategic partnerships

      CitiusTech scaled to more than twenty global locations and seven thousand technologists, earning trust from over 140 healthcare and life sciences enterprises. We strengthened our technology investments across AI, Cloud, Interoperability, and modern infrastructure. Bain Capital and BPEA brought further momentum to our expansion. 

      2024 - 2025

      Industry leadership and AI-driven transformation

      We advanced our AI-first strategy and continued to be recognized for excellence, with the Minsky Award, AWS Industry Partner of the Year, and Gold Stevie Awards. We launched CitiusTech AI to accelerate intelligence across the healthcare ecosystem. 

      2025 - 2026

      AI-enabled platforms and scalable innovation

      We introduced CitiusTech Knewron, our healthcarenative AI platform, and expanded our capabilities with the acquisition of Health Data Movers. Multiple global awards recognized our technology excellence. We launched the CitiusTech AI Lab and HealthSPARX to unlock realworld data at scale. CitiusTech continued to be recognized as a Great Place to Work across India and the United States.
