About Shoprite Holdings 
Shoprite Holdings Ltd, an investment holding company, engages in the food retail and wholesale business in South Africa and internationally. It operates through the Supermarkets RSA, Supermarkets Non-RSA, and Other Operating segments. The company offers food, clothing, general merchandise, cosmetics, and liquor products under the Checkers, Checkers Hyper, and Usave brands.
