News for the New Year: GDPR Update for Employers
 January 16, 2019 
When the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) took effect in May 2018, there were open questions about how some of its provisions would be interpreted, what additional requirements might be adopted by European Union member countries, and the degree of enforcement. Join attorneys from Ogletree Deakins’ Data Privacy and International practice groups for an update on these and other issues, including tips and best practices for ensuring continuing compliance.
