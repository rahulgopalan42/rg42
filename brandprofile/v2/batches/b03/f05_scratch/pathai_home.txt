Introducing AISight, the Digital Pathology Image Management System from PathAI
AISight® is a cloud-native, open platform enterprise workflow solution used by the world's leading laboratories and research centers to power their digital pathology workflows and AI applications. It serves as a central hub for case management, image management, and best-in-class artificial intelligence tools to enable multiple histopathology use cases.
