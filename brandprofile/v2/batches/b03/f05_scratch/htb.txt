Who we are
Hackers at heart
Hack The Box is the leading cyber readiness platform for the human-AI cyber workforce, helping organizations develop and measure the capabilities of the people and AI agents responsible for modern defense. The platform delivers threat-informed learning paths, real-world labs and live-fire simulations that build and validate offensive, defensive and AI-enabled cyber capabilities. Trusted by the Fortune 500, government agencies, managed security service providers (MSSPs) and Frontier Labs, Hack The Box supports more than 800 enterprise customers and a global community of over 5 million cybersecurity professionals.

Meet Our Leadership
HARIS PYLARINOS
Founder & CEO
HARIS PYLARINOS
Founder & CEO
Haris is the Founder and CEO of Hack The Box. Driven by a vision to connect and upskill the global cybersecurity community, he disrupted the industry by bringing HTB to life. He has led the company’s global expansion, driving significant growth. Under his leadership, HTB has scaled to more than 300 employees and over 4 million platform members since its launch in 2017.
NIKOS FOUNTAS
Chief Operating Officer
NIKOS FOUNTAS
Chief Operating Officer
Nikos Fountas is COO, Company Director, Board Member, and an early team member at Hack The Box. With 15+ years of experience, he specializes in operations, strategy, and international B2B/B2G growth. He drives operational excellence, scalable processes, and customer-centric initiatives that support sustainable growth.
GIBB WITHAM
President
GIBB WITHAM
President
Gibb Witham is the President of Hack The Box. With two decades of experience across cybersecurity, AI, and enterprise software, Gibb has spent his career at the intersection of innovation, capital, and operational execution. Prior to Hack The Box, he spent 14 years as a venture capital investor at Paladin Capital Group, where he partnered with high-growth companies at the forefront of cybersecurity and enterprise AI innovation.
CHRISTINE BARTLETT
Chief Marketing Officer
CHRISTINE BARTLETT
Chief Marketing Officer
Christine Bartlett is CMO at Hack The Box, championing a simple idea: great cybersecurity teams aren’t born in classrooms, they’re forged through continuous, community-validated skills development. She turns HTB’s mission into momentum, shaping the brand and driving growth. With 12+ years in cybersecurity, including senior roles at Cisco and SonicWall, Christine is known for sharp go-to-market leadership, crisp executive comms, and making marketing measurable, earning a spot on Pavilion’s “50 CMOs to Watch in 2024.”
GERASIMOS MARKETOS
Chief Product Officer
GERASIMOS MARKETOS
Chief Product Officer
Gerasimos is CPO at Hack The Box, where he leads product strategy and execution, driving the evolution of hands-on learning, enterprise readiness, and emerging areas such as AI security. With over 20 years of experience across high-growth startups and NASDAQ-listed companies, he brings deep expertise at the intersection of cybersecurity, data, and product development, focusing on building scalable platforms and translating complex technologies into customer value.
KONSTANTINOS DOLKAS
Chief Technology Officer
KONSTANTINOS DOLKAS
Chief Technology Officer
Konstantinos Dollkas is CTO at Hack The Box, where he leads Infrastructure, Engineering, AI, Data, and Analytics, shaping the technology strategy behind the company’s next phase of growth. He plays a key role in scaling the capabilities that power the platform, reinforcing HTB’s commitment to innovation, performance, and operational excellence.
TOM FERNANDEZ
Chief Revenue Officer
TOM FERNANDEZ
Chief Revenue Officer
Tom Fernandez is CRO at Hack The Box, owning global revenue across sales, customer success, and sales engineering. A career operator in SaaS and cybersecurity, he has spent +13 years building and scaling markets across APAC and globally, with deep experience in enterprise GTM, channel motion, and revenue architecture in high-growth, efficiency-focused environments.
