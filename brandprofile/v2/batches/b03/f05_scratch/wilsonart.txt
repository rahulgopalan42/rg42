Wilsonart Timeline
Wilsonart is celebrating 70 years of entrepreneurship, work ethic, and passion. The story began in 1956 by producing laminate with 45 employees and a single press, growing to one of the world’s leading manufacturers and distributors of high-pressure laminate, quartz, solid surface, and other engineered surfaces.

Your legacy, more sustainable.
At Wilsonart, Mother Nature guides our purpose. Sustainability isn't an aspect of what we do. It's part of who we are. By creating better, stronger, more sustainable surfaces, we are engineering a better way.
