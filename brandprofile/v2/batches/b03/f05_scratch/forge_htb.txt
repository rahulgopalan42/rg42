Company details
Hack the Box is an edtech company designed to train individuals in cybersecurity penetration testing and methods. The private company uses a gamified platform with hands-on training experience all at different levels of experience. In addition to its training, Hack the Box also serves a community of experts who exchange ideas and knowledge on areas related to cybersecurity and its threats.
