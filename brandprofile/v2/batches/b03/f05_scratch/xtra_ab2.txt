 About XTRA 
Company Overview 
Based in St. Louis, MO and owned by Berkshire Hathaway, XTRA Lease is the leading provider of over-the-road trailers for rent and lease in the U.S. 
With about 50 locations, XTRA Lease offers a fleet of about 90,000 trailers for rent and lease, including dry vans, flatbeds, reefers, chassis, and storage trailers.
Our people are the cornerstone of our success. Every branch has a hard-working and responsive customer service and operations team that takes pride in delivering on our promise of excellence. They provide local focus with a nationwide reach. 
XTRA Lease Company History 
1957 – XTRA Corporation becomes the
 nation’s first piggyback-trailer leasing company.
1961 – XTRA Corporation becomes a public company
 listed on the New York Stock Exchange; listed as one of 20 companies in the
 Dow Jones Transportation Index.
1992 – XTRA Corporation creates the XTRA Lease
 division as a national
 company.
1997 – XTRA Lease introduces industry-changing
 Repair Cost Standards. The often-contentious issue of damage charges is
 lessened thanks to technology that standardizes the approach to
 estimating, communicating and charging customers for damage to rented or
 leased trailers.
2000 – XTRA Lease becomes the first trailer
 rental and leasing company to offer GPS- and cellular-based trailer
 tracking. 
2001 – On September 20, 2001, Berkshire Hathaway, headed by the “Oracle of Omaha”, Warren Buffett,
 acquires all assets of XTRA Corporation.
2005 – XTRA Lease introduces XTRA Xpress®,
 a mobile technology solution that gives fleets instant electronic
 notification of rental activity and real-time access to rental
 information.
2011 – XTRA Lease becomes the first trailer rental
 and leasing company to install fuel-efficient, aerodynamic side skirts on
 new reefers and dry vans. Combined with low rolling-resistance tires, the
 side skirts offer fleets a 6% savings in fuel efficiency, according to the
 EPA.
2017 – XTRA Lease introduces a new, more powerful
 trailer tracking service and portal. All dry vans and reefers are equipped
 with solar-powered, long-lived tracking units. Trailer tracking is
 still a free service.
2018 – XTRA Lease adds cargo sensors on all new dry
 vans as a free service, enabling fleets to improve turn time and collect
 valuable detention data.
2022 – XTRA Lease launches myXTRA™, a
 best-in-class customer portal with tracking, advanced reporting and alert
 features. Reefer telematics are included as a free service on 2022 and
 newer models.
2024 – XTRA Lease expands Texas presence with new
 Austin location.
2025 – XTRA Lease makes trailer tracking standard on
 all new flatbeds and adds solar charging to new liftgate vans and reefers.
XTRA Lease Culture and Customer Commitment 
Our customer commitment is reflected in the way we build our fleet and in the services we offer.
XTRA Lease trailers are equipped with best-in-class features and technology, producing a fleet that exceeds what you might expect from rental trailers. 
We support our equipment with flexible services and a rigorous maintenance program across the largest trailer rental network in the U.S. 
We’re proud of our legacy of reliability, service, and innovation. When you rent or lease with XTRA Lease, you’re working with the industry leader, a supplier you can trust.
We’re always looking for energetic, goal-oriented people with great customer focus and fresh ideas. Check out the positions at our national network of branches and our St. Louis headquarters.
