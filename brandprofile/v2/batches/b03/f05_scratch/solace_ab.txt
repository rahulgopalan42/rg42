ABOUT SOLACE
Health is deeply personal. 
Modern healthcare isn’t.
In 2016, our cofounder Jeremy’s mom was diagnosed with pancreatic cancer. She was an accomplished radiologist—but even with her deep knowledge of the medical system, her diagnosis was overwhelming. She needed help getting into trials, getting second opinions, weighing treatment options and weighing outcomes. 
Through her diagnosis, Jeremy was able to witness firsthand how difficult it is for anyone, no matter their background, to interact with the medical system. Healthcare in the US has become just as complex as the legal system, and most people (even doctors!) don't have the medical literacy to navigate any sort of health issue without an advocate.
We started Solace to change the landscape of healthcare education in the US. We believe that the expertise that advocates provide is a major step toward improving American healthcare, and with Solace, we provide an easy way for patients in need to find and work with advocates. 
Solace is meticulously designed to be a better healthcare experience. We not only listen to your needs and match you with an advocate who can help, but we accompany you through your entire healthcare journey. No health concern is simple (wouldn't that be nice?)—but no health concern is too complex for an advocate to solve. 
Solace is a platform of doers. Our advocates don't just tell you what to do and wish you the best. Solace Advocates make the phone calls, endure the hold music, chase down all the members of your medical team, and communicate across the board to make sure you get better health outcomes you deserve. They know the right people to talk to—and the right words to say—to untangle even the most complicated healthcare issues.
Everyone deserves the power to make their own decisions, especially when it comes to their health. At Solace, we connect people with experts who help them navigate, understand, and take charge of their healthcare journeys.
