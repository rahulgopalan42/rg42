About Us
Wilsonart engineered surfaces are the backgrounds of life. Whether in high-traffic or everyday spaces, our materials are crafted to live up to it all. For 70 years, we’ve helped architects, designers, and fabricators turn ideas into reality through surfaces that perform, service that delivers, and people who care.
For 70 years, we’ve built spaces and solutions designed for the rhythm of living.
Our hardworking products are crafted for the look, feel, and performance today’s spaces demand. Whether in high-traffic commercial environments or everyday interiors, we deliver more than surfaces, we bring the insight, reliability, and service of a 70-year legacy brand to every interaction. 
Wilsonart keeps projects moving, backing the architects and designers who bring ideas to life, supporting the fabricators who make them real, and serving the people who live, work, and gather in those spaces every day.
Wilsonart lives up to life.
