40th anniversary
Throughout a decade marked by numerous changes in the construction sector, the BigMat chain has known how to maintain its benchmark position thanks to its growth and internationalization based on a co-operation model with a view to building in the long term.
By keeping its course and its values, BigMat has been able to revolutionize itself on the foundation more than ever its cornerstone values: the spirit of service, excellence, sharing and solidarity, in order to consolidate its place as the leading international chain of independent traders in construction materials.
The BigMat group is proud to celebrate its 40 years of existence and is grateful to all of its members and employees, without forgetting all of its privileged partners.
