 Italmatch Chemicals signs supply agreement with the Saudi Water Authority 
03 September - 2026
 Corporate 
 Press Release 
Italmatch Chemicals is pleased to announce the signing of a long-term supply agreement with the Saudi Water Authority (SWA) during a ceremony held yesterday in Riyadh. The agreement was ratified in the presence of Group CEO and Founder of Italmatch Chemicals Sergio Iorio, along with His Excellency the President of the Saudi Water Authority Abdullah Bin Ibrahim Al-Abdulkareem, His Excellency the Minister of Environment, Water and Agriculture Abdulrahman Bin Abdulmohsen Al-Fadley, His Excellency the Minister of Investment Fahad Bin Abduljalil Al-Saif, and Their Excellencies the heads of various authorities.
This signing further consolidates Italmatch Chemicals’ footprint in the Middle East, while strengthening its position in water treatment, a strategic market segment for the Group. SWA is the Saudi governmental authority responsible for regulating and overseeing the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia water sector, with a mandate to ensure long-term water security in line with Saudi Vision 2030. The new agreement reinforces the two organizations’ shared commitment to developing local technologies for water treatment applications.
