This is a list of subsidiaries, equities, cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments owned by multinational holding company Berkshire Hathaway.
Cash, cash equivalents and U.S. Treasury Bills[edit]
As of September 30, 2026, Berkshire Hathaway had $352.3 billion in cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments in U.S. treasury bills.[1]
