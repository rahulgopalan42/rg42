About Guttman Holdings
Guttman Holdings, Inc.
Our 100% Employee-Owned Company
In 2022, Guttman Energy, Inc., Source One Transporation, LLC, and Guttman Renewables, LLC united to form Guttman Holdings, Inc. as part of an Employee Stock Ownership Plan (ESOP).
As a 100% employee-owned company, each of our employee owners has a financial stake in our customers’ satisfaction and our company’s growth and success. As employee owners, we live by our core values and rally behind our common purpose:
“We fuel our customers’ success so that each of our employee owners and their families can prosper.”
EMPLOYEE OWNERSHIP MATTERS
How Shared Success Creates Lasting Value
ESOPs − like Guttman Holdings, Inc. − produce a culture of committed and empowered employees, who are deeply motivated to serve their customers, and their communities. Guttman Holdings’ employee owners are directly invested in the success of our 100% employee-owned company. As a result, our customers and the communities we serve also reap the benefits. Here’s how.

Whether you’re a current or prospective employee owner of Guttman Energy or Source One Transportation, one of our customers, or a member of one of the communities we operate in, there are clear benefits that make employee ownership a win-win for all. When our customers and our company succeed, our ESOP grows, our communities and local economies strengthen, and each of our employee owners and their families can thrive.
