About Us 
A Vision for Sustainable Innovations 
Nurturing a world fueled by nature’s energy  
Transforming the Future 
 Born with a vision to transform the Indian energy landscape, HPCL-Mittal Energy Limited (HMEL) was formed in 2007 through a prestigious joint venture between Fortune 500 company Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Limited and Mittal Energy Investments Pte. Ltd. We aspire to enrich everyday lives and bring happiness to countless individuals through diversified products, ranging from essential fuels and derivatives to high-quality polymers. Our journey took a significant leap forward with the commissioning of the Guru Gobind Singh Refinery in 2012 in Bathinda, one of India’s most energy-efficient refineries. Since then, HMEL has emerged as a visionary architect of a brighter future, leading the way with innovation and advanced materials to create a lasting positive impact that resonates for generations to come. 
Who We Are
We are a young, dynamic, and forward-thinking organisation with a mission to empower businesses and people by shaping the future of energy. Our goal is to drive progress through our world-class fuels and petrochemical products in a safe and sustainable manner.
What We Do
We use natural resources and cutting-edge technology  to meet the energy demands of today and tomorrow. From producing high-quality petroleum products to creating polymers vital for critical growth industries, we move beyond conventions with a focus on solar and renewable energy to reduce our carbon footprint.
What We Believe
At HMEL, we believe that progress and purpose should go hand in hand for a responsible, positive change. Every decision we make embodies our commitment to sustainability, innovation, and safety, ensuring that each step forward is taken with care for both the planet and its people.

Energising India’s Growth Story
India is the world’s third-largest energy consumer, and its energy needs are set to surge with rapid development. Meeting this demand will require leveraging all available energy sources, from legacy to innovative alternatives. 
As India’s leading oil and gas company, we are uniquely positioned to meet the nation’s evolving energy needs. HMEL owns and operates the Integrated Refinery-Petrochemical Complex, Guru Gobind Singh Refinery, in Bathinda, Punjab, comprising  an 11.3 MMTPA Crude Oil Refinery and a 1.2 MMTPA Multi-Feed Cracker along with 1.2 MMTPA Polyethylene (PE) and 1.0 MMTPA Polypropylene (PP) Plants. The synergy between our refinery and petrochemical operations reflects our adaptive strategy, allowing swift responses to market demands. With smart automation and eco-friendly practices, we play a pivotal role in India’s energy and economic transformation journey, producing both fuels and high-value petrochemicals that power industries and millions of lives.

Our Core Values: 
The Pillars of Our Success 
HMEL has cultivated a performance-driven culture rooted in six core values that guide every decision we make, every action we take, and every interaction we have. 
Safety First 
We place safety above all. With an unrelenting focus on protecting human life and resources, our processes ensure proactive planning ahead and taking responsibility to lead by example.  
Teamwork 
Together, we achieve the extraordinary. Teamwork is at the heart of our success, as we unite to overcome challenges, celebrate victories, and create a supportive environment where we care for each other.  
Achieve Targets and Meet Deadlines 
At HMEL, delivering on our promises with precision is non-negotiable. Through careful planning and timely decision-making, we meet every deadline with excellence and utmost accountability. 
Respect for People 
We are a people-centric organisation that fosters an inclusive environment where every voice is heard, every individual is respected, and every contribution is valued.   
Continuous Improvement and Learning 
At HMEL, growth is a constant. Our culture of continuous learning and improvement keeps us ahead of the curve, inspiring our people to constantly elevate their skills and performance. 
High Ethical Standards 
Integrity and ethical leadership defines who we are. Upholding HMEL’s code of conduct and staying true to our principles, we act with honesty and transparency in all we do. 
Our Promoters: 
At the core of HPCL-Mittal Energy Limited (HMEL) is a dynamic alliance between two industry powerhouses. Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Ltd (HPCL), a Fortune Global 500 Public Sector Undertaking, is a frontrunner in India’s oil refining and marketing landscape, operating major refineries in Mumbai and Visakhapatnam. Partnering with HPCL is Mittal Energy Investments Pte Ltd, owned by Mr. Lakshmi N. Mittal, contributing strategic vision and global energy expertise. With each partner holding close to a 49% stake, HMEL stands as a beacon of innovation, operational excellence, and sustainable energy solutions—driving progress in India and across global markets. 
Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Ltd. 
A Fortune 500 and Forbes Global 2000 company, Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Limited (HPCL) is a Government of India Enterprise and Maharatna status. As one of India’s largest oil refining and marketing companies, HPCL owns around 24% of the country’s fuel retail outlets. The corporation operates two major refineries in Mumbai and Visakhapatnam and holds a significant stake in Mangalore Refinery and Petrochemicals Ltd., with a combined capacity of 25.27 MMTPA, making it a key player in India’s economy with a substantial global impact. 
Mittal Energy Investments  
Mittal Energy Investments Pte. Ltd., a 100% subsidiary of Mittal Investments Sarl (a Luxembourg-incorporated company), is a key player in the global oil and gas sector. Beneficially owned by Mr. Lakshmi N. Mittal and his family, the company acquires and holds interests in enterprises related to oil and gas, and infrastructure projects.
