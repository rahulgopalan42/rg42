About Droom
Over the last decade, Droom has built the largest and most comprehensive digital ecosystem for automobiles in India. What began as a pure-play used vehicle marketplace is now a full-scale Mobility Super App, transforming how India buys, sells, rents, insures, finances, maintains, and plans everything around automobiles.
We’ve evolved from being a once-in-six-years transaction platform (used car buying/selling) into an everyday app that touches the customer up to 90 times over six years, unlocking over $5,000 in revenue per user every six years —compared to just $450–$1,000 before.
This shift not only increases engagement and monetization but also solves the inherent limitations of used automobile E-Commerce in India, which has seen just 0.4% online penetration after 10 years—far below the 10% we initially projected in 2014.
 Rental: Tech-first, short-term, driver-driven rentals with focus on EVs and premium experience ($16.5B TAM vs $6.5B for on-demand ride-hailing).
 Buy & Sell: End-to-end E-Commerce + listings, seller/buyer/closing services—removing friction and increasing TAM.
 Droom EV: Zero emission distribution online, enabling buying and selling of pre-owned EVs, battery management, insurance, loans, maintenance, and recycling.
 Loan & Insurance: Seamless financing and coverage options.
 Care: Full vehicle lifecycle support, including inspections, warranties, repairs, Fastag, challans, RC transfers, detailing, and even driver-on-demand.
 MyDroom (AI-Powered Predictive Planning Tools): Our proprietary AI engine leverages petabytes of data from a decade of operations to deliver predictive insights—from vehicle health and repair forecasts to insurance renewals and smart mobility planning.
 Research: A data-backed research layer offering market trends, pricing intelligence, ownership insights, and decision frameworks for consumers, enterprises, and ecosystem partners.
Droom is no longer just a used car marketplace. We’re a tech-and AI-driven mobility ecosystem—digitally managing the entire lifecycle of every vehicle in the country.
 We are currently at $600mil GMV and $30mil annual run rate of revenue, but will likely end up at $50mil in the next six months due to two possible acquisitions we are closing. The company has over 20 tech and AI products and 13 US patents. We plan to file our IPO by the the end of next year in India with a target valuation of $1.5bn.
 Sandeep Aggarwal is the Founder & CEO at Droom. Sandeep Aggarwal is a serial tech entrepreneur. Sandeep is the only tech founder to have founded two Unicorns back-to-back. Sandeep founded ShopClues in 2010 in his house in Silicon Valley as India’s first ever marketplace independent of any category to empower 30 million SMEs, and Droom as the first pure play E-Commerce for automobiles in India in 2014. Before his entrepreneurial journey, Sandeep was among the top-ranked Wall Street analysts covering the the global internet sector based out of Silicon Valley and worked with Schwab and Microsoft in their global headquarters in the USA, working with legendary founders such as Chuck Schwab and Bill Gates. Sandeep is the author of Fall Again and Rise Again, a best-selling book on the journey of an entrepreneur with a humble beginning, and he appeared as a judge on MTV Dropout, India’s first-ever reality show on TV to promote entrepreneurship. Sandeep has an MBA from Washington University in St. Louis and holds a US patent.
