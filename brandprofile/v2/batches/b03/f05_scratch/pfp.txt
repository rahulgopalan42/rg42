 About Us 
We develop, manage, and support thriving Communities, across the country. 
Let us introduce ourselves...
We are Places for People, the UK's leading Social Enterprise. We change lives by creating and supporting thriving Communities. 
Our business comprises complementary companies that are market leaders in homes and Communities, regeneration and development, investment management, property management and leisure. Working together, we use our expertise and reach to create and manage entire Communities, providing homes, services support, and infrastructure that enable our Customers and their Communities to thrive. 
Who we are
As the UK’s leading Social Enterprise, we engage and utilise all parts of our group to create and support thriving Communities across the UK.
We take a commercial approach that centres a whole-Community focus at its core. By managing and developing social housing, looking right across our organisation, and building partnerships, we have a model that aims to be more than the sum of our parts.
Our unwavering mission is to enrich the Communities that we are privileged to serve.

Our social impact
We believe it’s our responsibility to ensure our products and services positively impact our Communities, Customers, and the Climate. That’s why creating social value is at the heart of our approach.
