 The original malpractice insurance 
 Since 1899, MedPro Group has set the standard in medical malpractice insurance. With each passing decade, we've innovated, defended, and persevered, forging a legacy of trust that spans more than a century. We're honored that millions of providers have put their trust in us. 
 Our story 
Part of Berkshire Hathaway
It’s a name known around the world, and one that we’re proud to be a part of. In 2005, we joined Warren Buffett’s Berkshire Hathaway, which has helped us maintain the industry’s highest financial ratings (A++ from AM Best). Our insureds have true peace of mind knowing we'll be there for them. 
Warren Buffett, Berkshire Hathaway Chairman Emeritus“For more than 100 years, MedPro has been the ‘gold standard’ in protecting and defending the assets and reputations of healthcare providers.”
