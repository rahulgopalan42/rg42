METHOD AND DECISIONS
- Every list name went through check.py (DISC "check" field). check.py misses many Taiwanese listed companies (it answers NEW for
  MediaTek, Acer etc.), so DISC "NEW" for Taiwan does NOT mean unlisted; listing must be checked before research.
- NZ Top 200 entries with an exchange ticker on the page are marked LISTED / left out.
- Crown Resorts left out: established serious compliance problems (NSW inquiry found it unsuitable and that it facilitated money
  laundering; Victorian royal commission).
- Skyline Enterprises meets Route A on Deloitte revenue but its owner was not confirmed on a page: kept in WATCH.
- Palit moved to MAIN (Route C) once Wikipedia confirmed it is private.
- Gong Cha now has its HQ in London and was bought by Bain Capital (Aug 2026, Wikipedia): outside this country scope, not researched.
- Squadron Energy (Tattarang) and Viu were researched by other batch-3 researchers: not repeated.
- Taiwan Sugar shows "traded_as 1237.TW" on Wikipedia; listing status unclear, not researched.
- Land Bank of Taiwan has a TWSE stock code for reporting but states "Overseas Listings: None"; it is 100% state (Ministry of Finance
  directors); qualified on profit before tax.
- QNET: route met through its Manchester City and CAF sponsorships, but its own site carries scam and misinformation advisories;
  flagged in "compliance", confidence low.
- Lotto New Zealand, Land Bank, Rest and ART qualify financially but have weak F1 fit (domestic only); fit scores reflect that.
