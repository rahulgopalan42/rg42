'''Taoyuan International Airport Corporation''' ('''TIAC'''; {{zh|t=桃園國際機場公司|s=桃园国际机场公司|first=t|p=Táoyuán Guójì Jīchǎng Gōngsī}}) is a [[government-owned corporation]] responsible for the management of [[Taiwan Taoyuan International Airport]]. It is a company based in [[Taoyuan City|Taoyuan]], Taiwan, [[Republic of China]], established on November 1, 2010.<ref>{{cite news|title=Taoyuan airport to enter new era as a state-run firm|url=http://www.taipeitimes.com/News/taiwan/archives/2010/11/01/2003487445|accessdate=10 March 2018|work=Taipei Times|date=1 November 2010}}</ref>
