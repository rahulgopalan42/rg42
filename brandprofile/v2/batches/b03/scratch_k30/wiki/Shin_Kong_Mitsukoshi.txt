'''Shin Kong Mitsukoshi''' (SKM, {{zh|t=新光三越百貨|p=Xīnguāng Sānyuè Bǎihuò}}) is a Taiwanese brand of high-end [[department stores]] operating throughout [[Taiwan]], with concessions also located in mainland [[China]]. The brand is a joint venture of the Taiwanese [[Shin Kong Group]] and Japanese [[Mitsukoshi]].<ref>{{cite news |url= https://www.taipeitimes.com/News/biz/archives/2019/10/18/2003724134|title= Shin Kong Mitsukoshi’s annual sale off to strong start |last= Kwan|first= Shin-han |date= 2019-10-18 |publisher= Taipei Times |accessdate= 2024-05-31}}</ref>

Shin Kong Mitsukoshi's first store was opened in 1991 in [[Zhongshan District, Taipei]] and there are now 16 branches across Taiwan. The first Shin Kong Mitsukoshi concession in China opened in [[Beijing]] in April 2007.

== Incidents ==
On 13 February 2025, four people were killed and 30 injured at the Taichung branch of SKM after a [[2025 Taichung Shin Kong Mitsukoshi gas explosion|natural gas explosion]] occurred on the 12th floor.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Hsiao |first=Po-yang |date=2025-02-13 |title=4 dead, 20 injured in Taichung mall blast |url=https://focustaiwan.tw/society/202502130009 |access-date=2025-02-13 |website=Focus Taiwan |language=en-US}}</ref><ref name="Focus">{{Cite web |last=Hao |first=Hsueh-ching |date=2025-02-13 |title=Taichung department store ordered to suspend operations after fatal explosion |url=https://focustaiwan.tw/society/202502130021 |access-date=2025-02-13 |website=Focus Taiwan |language=en-US}}</ref> Two people were killed directly by the explosion, while two tourists from [[Macau]] were killed by falling debris on the street.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Hao |first=Hsueh-ching |date=2025-02-13 |title=2 members of Macau family dead in Taichung mall explosion |url=https://focustaiwan.tw/society/202502130016 |access-date=2025-02-13 |website=Focus Taiwan |language=en-US}}</ref> Taichung mayor [[Lu Shiow-yen]] ordered the branch to suspend operations pending a safety inspection.<ref name="Focus" /><ref>{{cite news |url=https://www.scmp.com/news/china/politics/article/3298493/4-dead-and-least-10-injured-taichung-department-store-blast-taiwan-media |title=2 Macau residents among 4 dead in Taiwan department store blast |first1=Sylvie |last1=Zhuang |first2=Lawrence |last2=Chung |newspaper=[[South China Morning Post]] |date=13 February 2025 |access-date=13 February 2025}}</ref> The store reopened on 27 September 2025 following renovations.<ref>{{Cite web |date=27 September 2025 |title=Shin Kong Mitsukoshi Taichung reopens after gas explosion closure |url=https://focustaiwan.tw/society/202509270013 |access-date=27 September 2025 |website=Focus Taiwan}}</ref>
