'''Gong Cha''' ({{lang-zh|t={{linktext|貢|茶}}|p=Gòngchá}}) is a tea drink [[franchising|franchise]] founded in 2006 in [[Kaohsiung]], [[Taiwan]].<ref name=GCglobal>{{cite web |title=Our Story: The Highest Quality |url=https://www.gong-cha.com/web/story |website=gong-cha. |access-date=16 July 2021 |archive-date=24 February 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230224085247/https://www.gong-cha.com/web/story |url-status=dead }}</ref><ref name=GCUSA>{{cite web |title=About Gong Cha |url=https://www.gongchausa.com/about-us |website=gongchausa. |access-date=16 July 2021 |archive-date=21 January 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220121205413/https://www.gongchausa.com/about-us |url-status=dead }}</ref><ref name=GCCanada>{{cite web |title=Gong-Cha: Our Tea Story |url=https://gong-cha.ca/our-story/ |website=gong-cha.ca |access-date=16 July 2021 }}</ref>

== Name ==
The name is a combination of the [[Chinese character]]s "{{linktext|貢}}", meaning contribution or tribute, and "{{linktext|茶}}", referring to tea.<ref>{{Cite web|date=January 2016|title=Gongcha Daily Tea Place|url=http://sampartners.co.kr/en/portfolio-item/gongcha/?ckattempt=1|access-date=21 June 2021|website=sampartners|archive-date=24 June 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210624202347/http://sampartners.co.kr/en/portfolio-item/gongcha/?ckattempt=1|url-status=dead}}</ref>

== History ==
Gong Cha, a tea company, was founded in 2006 in [[Kaohsiung]], [[Taiwan]]. Its first store in Hong Kong was established in 2009 by a [[Hong Kong]] native who had lived in Taiwan for five years.<ref name="TheSun20090608">{{cite news |date=2009-06-08 |title=台飲過江龍 奶泡滑到黏嘴 (Taiwan-style drink 'River-Crossing Dragon': foam so smooth it sticks to your mouth') |work=[[The Sun (Hong Kong)|The Sun]] |url=http://the-sun.on.cc/cnt/lifestyle/20090608/00479_001.html |access-date=12 December 2012}}</ref>

In 2012, the company had forty-six stores in Hong Kong, primarily located in [[MTR]] stations and shopping centers. However, due to various health and safety scandals,<ref>{{cite web |title=香港的營業據點 (Operating locations in Hong Kong) |url=http://www.gong-cha.com/location.php?cat_id=16 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141016013126/http://www.gong-cha.com/location.php?cat_id=16 |archive-date=2014-10-16 |publisher=Gong Cha}}</ref> the number of stores has decreased to just five. Gong Cha serves approximately fifty-seven different drinks, divided into seven types: house specials, brewed tea, [[milk tea]], Creative Mix, [[coffee]], Healthy Series, and ice [[smoothie]]s. These drinks are offered in medium and large sizes. Additionally, Gong Cha sells tea sets and various types of tea in stores, such as [[Biluochun|Bi-Luo-Chun]] Green Tea and [[Darjeeling tea|Darjeeling Spring Tea]]. Some locations also provide food options, including the recent addition of "Mochi Waffles."<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://gong-cha-usa.com/menu/|title=Drinks and Snacks}}</ref>

In 2017, 70% of ownership was sold to Kim Yeo-Jin ({{Korean|김여진|金汝貞|labels=no}}), owner of Gong Cha Korea, Gong Cha's Korea franchisee, with backing from private equity firm [[Unison Capital]].

In January 2017, Gong Cha's main headquarters was acquired by Gong Cha Korea. CEO Kim Soo-Min has announced plans to expand to the Middle East and Latin America.<ref>{{cite news |title="맛 빼고는 다 바꿔" 공차코리아 대수술, 본사까지 인수…글로벌 프랜차이즈 주인으로 |url=http://news.hankyung.com/article/2016112957571 |publisher=[[The Korea Economic Daily]] |date=2016-11-29 |access-date=2019-01-23 |archive-date=2019-01-24 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190124041619/http://news.hankyung.com/article/2016112957571 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

In 2019, [[TA Associates]] agreed to provide growth investment to Gong Cha and later bought 100% of Gong Cha Korea. UK company GC Group BIDCO Limited bought the remaining 30% in the same year, with GC Group BIDCO renamed to Gong Cha Global in 2020, moving the global headquarters to [[UK]].

In October 2025, it was reported that Gong Cha had closed all its outlets in Singapore.<ref name="CNA x309">{{cite web | last=Koh | first=Wan Ting | title=Bubble tea brand Gong Cha shuts all outlets in Singapore: Report | website=CNA | date=2 October 2025 | url=https://www.channelnewsasia.com/singapore/gong-cha-outlets-closed-singapore-5381391 | access-date=2 October 2025}}</ref> Gong Cha will return to Singapore in 2026 with a new franchise partner.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Dong |first=Irene |date=2025-10-03 |title='We'll be back': Gong Cha exits Singapore, but promises return |url=https://insideretail.asia/2025/10/03/well-be-back-gong-cha-exits-singapore-but-promises-return/ |access-date=2025-10-04 |website=Inside Retail Asia |language=en-US}}</ref>

[[Bain Capital]] announced in August 2026 that it had acquired Gong Cha for an undisclosed sum.<ref>{{Cite news |last=Lee |first=Jenny |date=2026-08-06 |title=Bain Capital to acquire bubble tea chain Gong Cha as rival MBK grapples with regulatory pressure at home |url=https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/06/bain-capital-to-buy-gong-cha-as-mbk-grapples-with-regulatory-pressure.html |url-status=live |archive-url=https://megalodon.jp/2026-0806-1604-05/https://www.cnbc.com:443/2026/08/06/bain-capital-to-buy-gong-cha-as-mbk-grapples-with-regulatory-pressure.html |archive-date=2026-08-06 |access-date=2026-08-06 |work=[[CNBC]]}}</ref> Industry reports indicate that the deal is valued at over $635 million.<ref>{{Cite web |last=MINJI |first=JIN |date=2026-08-06 |title=Bain Capital to acquire Gong cha for $635 million |url=https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/business/bain-capital-to-acquire-gong-cha-for-635-million/12812024 |access-date=2026-08-07 |website=koreajoongangdaily |language=en-US}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=writers |first=Nikkei staff |title=Bain Capital to buy Taiwan-originated Gong Cha chain for over $635m |url=https://asia.nikkei.com/business/business-deals/bain-capital-to-buy-taiwan-originated-gong-cha-chain-for-over-635m |access-date=2026-08-07 |website=Nikkei Asia |language=en}}</ref>
