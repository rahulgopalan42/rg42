'''Ovolo Hotels''' ({{zh|t=奧華酒店}}) is an independent hotel brand and serviced apartment owner-operator<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.ovolohotels.com/en/about-us/|title=Ovolo Hotels story in Hong Kong and Australia|publisher=Ovolohotels.com|accessdate=15 January 2015}}</ref> headquartered in [[Hong Kong]]. The hotel was founded by [[Girish Jhunjhnuwala]] and is a privately family-owned business under the Hind Group.

==History==
Ovolo Hotels<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://hongkongbusiness.hk/hotels-tourism/feature/ovolo-way|title=The Ovolo way {{!}} Hongkong Business|date=2017-01-07|access-date=2017-07-17}}</ref> started as a serviced-apartment company in 2002. Girish Jhunjhnuwala acquired a single office complex in Hong Kong's Central Business District and developed it into an upscale serviced apartment.<ref>{{Cite web |title=How Hong Kong-based Ovolo Hotel Group grew into 13 global properties that put a twist on five-star hospitality |url=https://www.tatlerasia.com/lifestyle/travel/ovolo-hotels-hong-kong-global-success |website=Tatler Asia}}</ref>

After the opening of its first [[serviced apartment]] in 2002, the group has expanded. It had eight properties as of 2014.<ref>{{cite web|title=Ovolo Hotels Open First Overseas Headquarters in Melbourne, Australia|url=http://www.hospitalitynet.org/news/4067946.html|website=Hospitality Net|accessdate=19 May 2017|language=en|date=25 November 2014}}</ref> It now has hotels in Canberra, Melbourne and Sydney.<ref>{{cite web|last1=Kelly|first1=Martin|title= Girish Jhunjhnuwala, Founder & In Hong Kong, Ovolo operates 4 hotels across [[Hong Kong Island]], with an addition of one remaining serviced apartment.{{citation needed|date=May 2026}} Since 2015, Ovolo has offered a live-in coworking space under what it calls the Mojo Nomad programme.<ref>{{cite web|last1=Springer|first1=Kate|title=Smart hotels where work meets play|url=https://edition.cnn.com/travel/article/smart-hotels-digital-nomads/index.html|website= [[CNN]] |publisher= [[Time Warner]] |access-date=19 May 2017|date=February 13, 2017}}</ref> In 2018 Ovolo purchased the New Acton located Hotel Hotel in Canberra, and renamed it Ovolo Nishi as of March.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Breheny |first=Emma |date=17 January 2018 |title=Canberra's Hotel Hotel sold to Ovolo Group |url=https://www.gourmettraveller.com.au/travel/travel-news/hotel-hotel-sold-to-ovolo-group-5368/ |access-date=10 April 2024 |website=Gourmet Traveller}}</ref> In 2020, Ovolo switched all of its hotels and restaurants to go [[Vegetarianism|vegetarian]] for 365 days.<ref>{{Cite web|last=Jelski|first=Christina|date=2020-10-27|title=Hold the meat: Ovolo Hotels going vegetarian-only for a year|url=https://www.travelweekly.com/Travel-News/Hotel-News/Hold-the-meat-Ovolo-Hotels-going-vegetarian-only-for-a-year|access-date=2021-06-19|website=Travel Weekly|language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|date=2021-06-17|title=Year of the Veg: Hotel group goes vegetarian for 365 days|url=https://www.usatoday.com/picture-gallery/travel/hotels/2021/06/17/hong-kong-based-ovolo-group-hotels-go-vegetarian-year/7729158002/|access-date=2021-06-19|website=USA Today|language=en}}</ref>
