# Private funding by emerging theme (compiled 16 Nov 2026)

All figures come from pages found in this session. Where a figure comes only from a search-result summary and not from a fetched page, it is marked "(snippet)". Data providers count differently, so compare figures only within one source.

**Context (Crunchbase):**
- **2025 global venture funding:** $425B, up 30% YoY. AI took $211B, up 85%. https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/funding-data-third-largest-year-2025/ (a later Crunchbase article revises 2025 to $440B).
- **Q3 2026:** $159B, down 25% QoQ and up 53% YoY. Q1–Q3 2026 reached $679B, a record. https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/q3-2026-global-startup-funding-ai-billion-dollar-rounds-exits-data/

## 1. AI model labs and AI applications / agents
- **Funding 2025:**
  - Foundational AI: $88.9B across 66 deals.
  - All AI: $211B (Crunchbase).
  - https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/foundational-ai-startup-funding-doubled-openai-anthropic-xai-q1-2026/
- **2026:**
  - Foundational AI raised $178B in Q1 alone (to 31 Mar).
  - All AI took $102B in Q3, 64% of global funding.
  - https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/q3-2026-global-startup-funding-ai-billion-dollar-rounds-exits-data/
- **Direction:** Up. Q1 2026 alone was double all of 2025. Q3 AI was $102B against $45B in Q3 2025 (https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/global-vc-funding/).
- **Largest 2026 rounds:**
  - OpenAI: $122B, Feb–Mar.
  - Anthropic: $65B in Q2, including a $50B round in May (https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/na-startup-funding-ma-shattered-records-ai-q2-2026/), plus $30B in Feb.
  - xAI: $20B, Jan.
  - Agents: Mirendil raised a $1B Series A in Nov and Sierra $950M (snippet: https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/biggest-startup-funding-rounds-agentic-ai-infrastructure-mirendil-mercor/).
- **Sponsor limits:** Not found.

## 2. AI infrastructure: data centres, GPU cloud / neocloud
- **Funding 2025:** PitchBook puts neocloud VC at $4.4B (snippet: https://pitchbook.com/news/articles/neoclouds-secure-record-funds-as-nvidias-huang-says-more-investment-required).
- **2026:**
  - PitchBook: neocloud VC $6.6B across 58 deals year to date (date not seen, snippet).
  - Crunchbase: data centres raised $10B+ in Q3 2026.
- **Direction:** Up 51% versus all of 2025 (PitchBook).
- **Largest 2026 rounds:**
  - Nscale: $3.36B convertible in Sep (https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/25/ahead-of-u-s-ipo-british-ai-neocloud-nscale-secures-3-36b-in-convertible-finacing/), and a $2B Series C in Mar.
  - Crusoe: $3B Series F in late Aug/early Sep (https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/biggest-funding-rounds-crusoe-fluidstack-multibillion-dollar-ai-infrastructure/).
  - Fluidstack: $1.5B, same window.
- **Sponsor limits:** CoreWeave and Nebius shares slid on payback worries, and PitchBook expects consolidation (snippets).

## 5. Defence technology
- **Funding 2025:** $9.6B, a record at the time (Crunchbase military / national security / law enforcement categories).
- **2026:** More than $14.6B by early June 2026. https://news.crunchbase.com/defense-tech/startup-venture-funding-all-time-record-ai-anduril/
- **Direction:** Up. 2026 passed 2025's full-year record within five months. PitchBook gives a different basis: $49.1B deal value in 2025 for Five Eyes countries (snippet: https://pitchbook.com/news/reports/q4-2025-defense-tech-vc-first-look).
- **Largest 2026 rounds:**
  - Anduril: $5B Series H, May.
  - Shield AI: $2B Series G, Mar.
  - Saronic: $1.75B Series D, Mar.
- **Sponsor limits:** PitchBook quotes an investor seeing frothy valuations "without any commercial traction" (snippet). Funding is concentrated in a few rounds.

## 6. Space and satellites
- **Funding 2025:**
  - Seraphim: $12.4B.
  - BryceTech: $10.9B.
  - ESPI: €11.7B, up 60% (all snippets via https://newspaceeconomy.ca/2026/04/30/start-up-space-investment-2025-and-the-new-shape-of-private-space-capital/).
- **2026:** Q3 2026 was a record $20.8B (Seraphim, via Reuters/BusinessDay), or $10.8B excluding Blue Origin's $10B. Q2 was $7.4B. https://www.businessday.co.za/world/international-companies/2026-10-14-space-start-ups-draw-record-208bn-in-new-funding/
- **Direction:** Up. Q3 is nearly triple Q2. Crunchbase: aerospace raised $10B+ in Q3.
- **Largest 2026 rounds:**
  - Blue Origin: $10B, Q3.
  - Hadrian: $1.37B Series D, Q3.
  - Stoke Space: $1B, Q3.
- **Sponsor limits:** Seraphim expects funding to moderate from Q3 levels.

## 7. Quantum computing
- **Funding 2025:** $4.1B, a record (Crunchbase).
- **2026:** $1.2B by 18 May 2026. https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/quantum-computing-startup-investment-data-quantinuum-ipo/
- **Direction:** Down, or behind 2025's pace as of May ("on track to come in below" 2025). No later total was found. Oratomic raised $475M later (snippet).
- **Largest 2026 rounds:**
  - Photonic: $200M, May.
  - QuantWare: $178M Series B, May.
  - Quantum Motion: $160M Series C, May.
- **Sponsor limits:** Crunchbase says quantum startups "haven't produced much in profits yet". Quantinuum has listed publicly.

## 8. Nuclear (fission/SMR) and fusion
- **Funding 2025:**
  - Fission: over $2.5B (Crunchbase, snippet: https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/public-private-nuclear-fission-funding-2025/).
  - Fission plus fusion: 2025 was a record year, but no total was given.
- **2026:**
  - Fission plus fusion: more than $6B as of 1 Oct 2026, an all-time high. https://news.crunchbase.com/clean-tech-and-energy/nuclear-startup-funding-up-public-markets-bearish/
  - Fusion: $4.48B in the 12 months to Jul 2026 (FIA), up 69% (snippet). https://www.power-eng.com/nuclear/record-4-48bn-funding-boomtime-for-fusion-industry/
- **Direction:** Up, "far ahead" of the same period in 2025.
- **Largest 2026 rounds:**
  - Commonwealth Fusion: $1B, Jul.
  - Valar Atomics: $1B in two Series B tranches.
  - Proxima Fusion: $518M, Jul.
- **Sponsor limits:**
  - Recent nuclear IPOs trade well below their peaks: X-energy is down about half, and Oklo is down about two-thirds.
  - Scalable fusion has not yet come online.
