# F1 Sponsorship Leads — 600 companies (August 2026)

**Files**
- `f1-leads-600-2026-08.csv` — the 600-lead list (import-ready for Clay/CRM).
- `f1-leads-bench-2026-08.csv` — 87 additional qualified leads held in reserve
  (overflow beyond 600 after balancing sectors).

**Brief:** companies that are promising Formula 1 sponsorship prospects, can
plausibly afford ~USD 3.5M per annum in sponsorship spend, have **200–5,000
employees**, are spread **across all major sectors**, and are **not existing
F1 sponsors**.

## How the list was built

1. **Exclusion list first.** 353 current F1 sponsor entities were compiled
   from F1's global partner/supplier roster, the full 2026 partner rosters of
   all 11 teams (including Cadillac and Audi), 2026 Grand Prix title sponsors,
   and F1 Academy / F2 / F3 sponsors — including parent companies.
2. **Sector sourcing.** 15 parallel research sweeps sourced ~50 candidates
   each across: enterprise SaaS, cybersecurity, AI & data, fintech & payments,
   trading/crypto/wealth, banking & insurance, logistics, energy & mobility,
   industrial & engineering, luxury & lifestyle, beverages/food/CPG,
   electronics/gaming/esports, travel & hospitality, betting & iGaming, and a
   mixed sweep (telecom, health & wearables, professional services, real
   estate & construction, automotive aftermarket).
3. **Qualification gates applied to every lead:**
   - Employee count 200–5,000 (LinkedIn-style estimate; borderline cases
     verified by search).
   - Affordability: annual revenue ≥ ~USD 250M, **or** ≥ USD 150M raised with
     a round in the last ~24 months, **or** profitable with a demonstrated
     sports-sponsorship track record ≥ USD 1M/yr.
   - No current F1 relationship (team, championship, GP, or supplier tier).
4. **Compile.** 743 raw candidates → 21 removed by the exclusion screen
   (conflicts the sourcing sweeps missed, e.g. Dynatrace, Freshworks, Circle,
   Gemini, Motul, Kering Eyewear, William Grant & Sons, Super Group), 35
   cross-sector duplicates removed → 687 clean → balanced round-robin cut to
   **600**, remainder to the bench file.

Conflicts caught **during** sourcing (already absent from the list) include:
Airwallex (McLaren), Groq (McLaren), Confluent (Racing Bulls), CoreWeave and
Mubadala (Aston Martin/Abu Dhabi GP), Rubrik (McLaren), Arctic Wolf (Alpine),
Vantage Markets and BingX (Ferrari), EOS (Sauber/Audi), Lavazza (Australian
GP), ZIM and ADNOC L&S (headcount over band). Petromin's lapsed Aston Martin
deal makes it a warm re-entry lead (kept).

## Caveats

- Employee counts and revenue/funding figures are research estimates gathered
  August 2026; verify before outreach (Clay enrichment on the shortlist is the
  natural next step).
- The F1 sponsor landscape moves fast — re-screen against fresh rosters
  before any campaign; three sourcing sweeps (luxury, electronics/gaming,
  mixed) completed their lists but had their final verification passes cut
  short, so their entries lean more on the compile-time exclusion screen.
- Betting & iGaming leads are subject to category restrictions (team-level
  deals only, market-by-market).
