# Under-the-radar sectors for F1 sponsorship (August 2026)

Sectors with little or no presence on the 2026 F1 sponsor roster (353 entities
checked across F1 global partners, all 11 team rosters, GP title sponsors, and
F1 Academy/F2/F3), but with the budgets and audience fit to buy at the
~USD 3.5M/yr tier. Ordered by strength of opportunity.

## Saturated categories to deprioritize
Cloud/enterprise software, cybersecurity, crypto exchanges & CFD brokers,
watches, spirits & beer, energy drinks, lubricants, logistics carriers,
airlines, hotels, banks, asset managers, HR/staffing, telecom carriers,
eyewear, sports nutrition — all have multiple incumbents on the 2026 roster,
meaning category-exclusivity clauses and higher prices.

## How to use this
Categories with zero incumbents mean (a) no team-level category conflicts —
every team can sell you the slot, and (b) first-mover "Official X of F1/team"
positioning at the cheapest it will ever be. The strongest three to test
first: insurance, consumer health/OTC, and ride-hailing/mobility — each has
dozens of in-band companies with proven sports-marketing behavior.
