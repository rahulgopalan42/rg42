# F1 Prospect Qualification Spec (v3 — 5 Oct 2026)

Supersedes v2 (18 Sep 2026) for every list generated from 5 Oct 2026 onward. Purpose of the
change: widen the funnel. Revenue is often unpublished, audience size is not a proxy for
ambition, and ownership type does not stop a brand buying exposure.

## Hard filters (must pass all)
1. Valuation above USD 1B: market cap, post-money valuation or enterprise value, dated and
   sourced. Family-, foundation- or employee-owned companies with no published valuation
   pass on scale evidence (revenue above USD 1B, or clear market-leader status); flag them.
2. Headcount 51 or more. No upper limit.
3. Capacity to spend about USD 2.5M a year: not in distress, cost-cutting, restructuring or
   wind-down. Pre-revenue companies pass if valued above USD 1B and funded within 24 months.
4. Not already sponsoring an F1 team, F1 itself, a driver or a Grand Prix, directly or via a
   parent or common owner.
5. Not under sanctions, on the US Entity List, or headquartered in Russia or Iran
   (contracting impossibility, not a judgement call).
6. Not already in the master, the expansion book or an earlier batch.

## Removed from v2 (now recorded as flags, never a reason to reject)
- Revenue bar. Record revenue when found; flag "revenue unknown" when not.
- Audience bar. Flag "concentrated customer base" where fewer than roughly 20 customers.
- Ownership restrictions. Public, PE, family, state, sovereign and royal ownership all pass.
  Flag "state-linked" for majority government ownership. PEP-linked owners pass with a flag.
- Gambling: pass with flag "gambling" (grid precedent exists).
- Alcohol: pass, no flag.

## Soft flags (record, never reject)
Valuation above 50x revenue; PE-owned four years or more; revenue unknown; concentrated
customers; pre-revenue; state-linked; PEP-linked; gambling; recent ownership change.

## Verdicts
- Yes: all hard filters pass, fit score 2 or more, a credible marketing motive.
- Stretch: passes hard filters with one real reservation (unpublished valuation, recent
  ownership change, capacity doubt, fit exactly 2 with weak angles).
- Low-value (shown in red): passes hard filters but unlikely to convert — fit score 0 or 1,
  or pre-revenue with no marketing function, or a pure B2B supplier with a concentrated
  customer base and no brand ambition, or a state entity with no consumer-facing brand.
- No: a hard filter failed. Name it.

Fit score 0-4, one point each: sells in three or more F1 markets; buyers affluent 25-54 or
senior decision makers; brand story maps to performance, technology, precision or global
ambition; category not already taken on the grid.

Priority score: Yes = 60 + fit x 8; Stretch = 40 + fit x 6; Low-value = 20 + fit x 4.
"Up-and-coming" marker: crossed USD 1B within 24 months, or revenue or headcount growing
above 40% a year.

## Output per company
Name, domain, HQ, ownership, what it sells and to whom, headcount band, revenue (dated,
sourced, or "unknown"), valuation (dated, sourced), latest raise, fit score, up-and-coming
marker, flags, verdict with one-line reason, best angle. Mark estimates "(est)".
