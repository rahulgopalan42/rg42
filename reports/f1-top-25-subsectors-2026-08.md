# Top 25 sub-sectors for F1 sponsorship conversion (August 2026)

Ranked for probability of closing a deal at the ~USD 3.5M/yr tier with
200–5,000-employee companies. Ranking = propensity to buy (category precedent
on the 2026 grid or live competitor pressure) × budget timing (funding/IPO
cycles) × open inventory (few incumbents blocking team-level exclusivity).
